# User Content Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This User Content Policy explains how testimonies, stories, prayer requests, and similar content (“User Content”) are handled on Seeking His Kingdom (“SHK”). It works together with the **Terms of Use**, **Privacy & Cookies Policy**, **Community Guidelines**, and **Disclaimers & Safety** page.

## 1. Purpose of This Policy

The **Community Guidelines** describe overall behavior and content standards for SHK.  

This User Content Policy focuses specifically on:

- how testimonies, stories, and long-form posts are shared and displayed;
- how visibility and anonymity options work;
- how SHK may format, curate, or retain User Content; and
- additional safety considerations for sensitive stories.

If there is any conflict, the **Terms of Use** remain the primary legal document.

## 2. Types of Stories and Visibility

SHK is built around testimonies and related content. Depending on how the Site develops, content may fall into one or more of these categories:

1. **Public testimonies**  
   - Visible to all visitors (including guests without accounts), unless they choose to hide sensitive content where available.  
   - Associated with your account handle or display name and, where applicable, a profile page.

2. **Account-linked but limited-visibility posts (if/when supported)**  
   - Some posts may be visible only to signed-in users or within specific areas of the Site.  
   - Even when visibility is limited, they remain subject to this Policy, the Community Guidelines, and moderation.

3. **Featured or curated testimonies**  
   - From time to time, SHK may highlight certain testimonies or excerpts in special sections (for example, “Featured Stories”).  
   - Featured content must still follow all rules and may be removed or changed if circumstances require.

In all cases, the visibility of your content is determined both by your settings and by SHK’s moderation and safety systems.

## 3. Names, Anonymity, and Identifying Details

We encourage honest stories but also want to protect privacy:

- **Your own name and identity**
  - You may choose to use your real first name, initials, or a pseudonym as your display name or handle, subject to the Community Guidelines.
  - Avoid handles or names that are crude, hateful, or misleading (e.g., impersonating someone else).

- **Other people’s names**
  - Focus on your own story. Avoid publicly naming alleged abusers, offenders, or other individuals in a way that could put them or you at legal or physical risk.
  - When talking about others, consider using general descriptions (e.g., “a family member,” “a mentor,” “a former partner”) instead of full names.

- **Organizations, churches, and ministries**
  - You may mention churches or ministries where relevant to your story, but SHK is not a review or complaint platform.
  - Posts that primarily target or attack specific churches or organizations may be removed or limited.

- **Minors and vulnerable individuals**
  - Take extra care when your story involves children or vulnerable adults.
  - Do not share full names, addresses, or details that could easily identify minors or vulnerable individuals without clear, appropriate consent.
  - Moderators and admins may edit, hide, or remove content to protect minors and vulnerable people.

## 4. Content Standards for Testimonies

We welcome:

- **Stories of God’s work**  
  - Salvation testimonies, moments of conviction and repentance, seasons of growth, and stories of God’s faithfulness.

- **Ongoing struggle with hope**  
  - Honest descriptions of current struggles (sin, doubt, mental health, addiction, etc.) when expressed in a way that seeks help, accountability, or prayer rather than glorifying sin.

- **Scripture-rooted reflections**  
  - Insights and applications that point people toward Jesus and His Word.

We do not allow:

- **Graphic or explicit detail**
  - Sexual content or descriptions intended to arouse.
  - Graphic depictions of abuse, violence, or self-harm.
  - Excessive detail that is likely to trigger or harm others.

- **Glorifying sin or harm**
  - Presenting sin, abuse, or self-harm as desirable, admirable, or something to imitate.
  - Sharing content that encourages others to engage in harmful or sinful behavior.

- **Targeted attacks**
  - Stories whose main purpose is to attack, shame, or discredit specific individuals or groups.
  - Content that stirs unnecessary division rather than pointing to Christ and His work.

Moderators and admins may ask you to edit, or may edit, hide, or remove content that crosses these lines.

## 5. Editing, Formatting, and Curating

To keep SHK readable, safe, and consistent with its purpose:

- **Light editing and formatting**
  - SHK may lightly edit User Content for:
    - formatting (paragraph breaks, headings, tags),
    - obvious spelling or minor grammar issues that affect clarity,
    - removal of clearly unnecessary identifying details or graphic parts.
  - We aim not to change the meaning or message of your story.

- **Substantive changes**
  - If more significant edits are needed for safety or clarity, we may:
    - ask you to revise and resubmit, or
    - temporarily hide the content until it is revised,
    - in some cases, remove the content and invite you to share a safer version.

- **Curation and highlighting**
  - SHK may group testimonies into categories or tags (e.g., “addiction recovery,” “answered prayer”).
  - SHK may feature certain stories or excerpts in specific areas of the Site (for example, a featured section), always within the scope of the license described in the **Terms of Use**.
  - Featuring a story does not imply endorsement of every detail, theological statement, or perspective expressed in that story.

## 7. Editing and Deletion by Users

Where the Site supports it:

- **What you can edit**
  - You may be able to edit certain parts of your own content (for example, title, body text, tags, and visibility settings) while it remains published.
  - Edits may still be subject to moderation, and older versions may be retained internally for safety and audit purposes.

- **How to request removal**
  - You may have tools to remove or unpublish your content directly.
  - If you cannot remove content yourself, you may contact us using the details on the **About & Contact** page and request removal, including:
    - a link to the content, and
    - a brief explanation of your request.

- **Limits and retention**
  - Removing or unpublishing content generally means it will no longer appear publicly on the Site.
  - SHK may still retain internal copies as described in the **Terms of Use** and **Privacy & Cookies Policy** (for example, in backups, safety logs, or legal records).

## 8. Retention and Archiving

In general:

- Testimonies and stories may remain publicly visible as long as:
  - your account is active,
  - the content complies with the Community Guidelines, and
  - you do not choose to remove or hide it.

For safety and compliance:

- SHK may retain internal records of:
  - moderated or removed content,
  - content associated with strikes, suspensions, or bans,
  - content needed to review past decisions or detect abuse patterns.

Older or sensitive content may be:

- **Archived or de-identified**
  - For example, removing or masking identifying details while preserving the core of the story for internal records or aggregated insights.
- **Further restricted in visibility**
  - For example, remaining in an account’s history but no longer appearing in public listings.

See the **Privacy & Cookies Policy** for more details on data retention.

## 9. Safety Considerations for Testimonies

Because testimonies sometimes include crisis-related topics:

- **Self-harm and suicide**
  - Content that encourages or normalizes self-harm or suicide is not allowed.
  - Honest descriptions of struggle may be allowed but could be:
    - marked as sensitive,
    - edited or limited,
    - prioritized for review by moderators or admins.

- **Abuse, violence, and ongoing danger**
  - You may share that you have experienced abuse or harm, but:
    - avoid graphic detail,
    - avoid publicly naming alleged abusers when possible.
  - If your story indicates that someone is currently in danger, moderators may prioritize review and may respond in ways allowed by the **Disclaimers & Safety** policy.

- **Reporting and support**
  - If you see content suggesting someone may be in immediate danger or serious crisis, you may:
    - report the content via the Site tools, and
    - encourage them (if appropriate) to seek local help and emergency services.

For more about how SHK responds to crisis-related content, please see the **Disclaimers & Safety** page.
