# Community Guidelines

_Last updated: [DATE]_

> These guidelines exist to help Seeking His Kingdom (“SHK”) remain a Christ-centered, safe, and honest place to share testimonies, prayer requests, and encouragement.

By using SHK, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines, together with the **Terms of Use**, **Privacy & Cookies Policy**, **User Content Policy**, and **Disclaimers & Safety** page.

---

## 1. Why These Guidelines Exist

- SHK exists to help people engage with Scripture, testimony, and practical resources for following Jesus.
- We want SHK to be a place of truth and honesty, but also grace, humility, and care for one another.
- These Guidelines explain, in plain language, what is and is not appropriate on the Site.  
- In the event of a conflict, the **Terms of Use** remain the primary legal document.

---

## 2. Core Values

We ask every user to keep these values in mind when posting and interacting:

- **Respect and dignity for every person**  
  Treat others as people made in the image of God (Genesis 1:27), even when you disagree.

- **Honesty and humility in sharing**  
  Be truthful about your story, acknowledge your own weaknesses, and avoid exaggeration or sensationalism.

- **Grace, encouragement, and edification**  
  Seek to build others up in love rather than to tear them down (Ephesians 4:29), and be slow to anger, quick to listen.

---

## 3. What to Share

SHK is primarily for:

- **Personal testimonies and stories**  
  How God has worked in your life, including salvation stories, seasons of growth, and ways He has carried you through difficulty.

- **Scripture-based encouragement and reflections**  
  Verses, insights, and reflections that aim to draw people closer to Christ, not to promote arguments or division.

- **Requests for prayer (as features become available)**  
  Honest prayer needs, shared with appropriate discretion and respect for others’ privacy.

When you share, remember that SHK is a public or semi-public space. Only share what you are comfortable having associated with your account.

---

## 4. What Not to Share

The following types of content are not allowed and may lead to removal, strikes, suspension, or a ban:

- **Harassment, hate, or threats**
  - Insults, bullying, or persistent targeting of an individual.
  - Hate speech, slurs, or demeaning language toward any group.
  - Threats of violence or harm (against yourself or others).

- **Explicit, graphic, or exploitative content**
  - Pornographic content or descriptions meant to arouse.
  - Graphic depictions of violence, abuse, or self-harm.
  - Sexualization of yourself or others.

- **Doxxing and privacy violations**
  - Sharing someone else’s private contact details, address, or other identifying information without clear consent.
  - Publicly naming alleged abusers or wrongdoers in ways that may create legal or safety issues, rather than focusing on your own story.

- **Spam, scams, or aggressive promotion**
  - Repeated promotion of products, services, donation links, or external ministries that distracts from the purpose of SHK.
  - Misleading schemes, fake stories, or deceptive fundraising.

- **Targeted attacks on individuals, churches, or ministries**
  - Posts whose main purpose is to attack or discredit a particular person, church, or organization.
  - Content that stirs division or unnecessary controversy rather than edifying others.

If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, consider rewriting it more carefully or leaving it out.

## 5. Handling Sensitive Topics

Many testimonies involve difficult topics such as trauma, abuse, sexual sin, self-harm, addiction, or mental health struggles. SHK wants to allow honest stories while protecting readers:

- Be **honest but wise**  
  Share enough to tell the truth about what God has done, without graphic or shocking details.

- **Protect privacy**  
  Avoid naming other people (especially alleged abusers) or giving details that would easily identify them. Keep the focus on what happened to you and how God has worked.

- **Avoid graphic detail**  
  Do not describe abuse, violence, or sexual content in graphic or explicit terms. If you are unsure, err on the side of restraint.

- **Sensitive content markings**  
  Some posts may be marked as _sensitive_ and:
  - Shown with a warning, or
  - Hidden for users who choose not to view sensitive content in their settings.

Additional guidance about sensitive topics, self-harm, and crisis content is provided on the **Disclaimers & Safety** page.

---

## 6. Promotion and External Links

SHK is not primarily a promotion or marketing platform. To keep the focus clear:

- Limited, relevant links (for example, to a church site, a specific sermon, or a helpful resource) may be allowed when they directly support your testimony or reflection.
- Repeated posting of links to the same site, channel, or product, especially when unrelated to your testimony, may be treated as spam.
- Promotion that appears to be primarily about gaining followers, donations, or customers rather than pointing to Christ may be removed.

If you are unsure whether something is acceptable, share your story first and consider leaving promotional details out, or ask an admin before posting.

## 7. Moderation & Consequences

SHK uses moderation to protect users and uphold these Guidelines:

- **Moderation actions may include**:
  - Quiet edits or formatting adjustments that do not change your meaning.
  - Hiding or removing specific posts or comments.
  - Marking content as sensitive.
  - Sending you warnings or guidance.
  - Temporarily limiting your ability to post.
  - Strikes on your account, which may lead to suspension or a ban in repeated or serious cases.

- **Repeat or severe violations**  
  Repeated or serious violations (for example, ongoing harassment, explicit content, or dangerous content) may result in:
  - Temporary suspension (read-only access),
  - Longer suspensions,
  - Permanent ban from using SHK.

Moderators and admins aim to act fairly and consistently, but SHK reserves the right to make final decisions on moderation in its sole discretion, as described in the **Terms of Use**.

## 8. How to Report a Concern

If you see content or behavior that appears to break these Guidelines or seems unsafe:

- **Use the report tools in the Site** (where available) to flag a specific post or account; or
- **Contact us** using the details provided on the **About & Contact** page, including:
  - A link or clear description of the content or account,
  - A short explanation of your concern.

Reports are used to:

- Prioritize content for review,
- Protect users and uphold the Community Guidelines,
- Improve moderation decisions over time.

Please do not abuse the report system (for example, mass-reporting someone just because you disagree with them). Misuse of reporting may itself lead to action on your account.

Additional information about safety, self-harm, and crisis-related content is provided on the **Disclaimers & Safety** page.
