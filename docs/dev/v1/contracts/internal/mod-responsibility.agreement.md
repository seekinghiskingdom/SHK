# SHK Moderator Responsibilities & Agreement (Draft v1)

> This document is an informal summary of how moderation is intended to work on Seeking His Kingdom (SHK) and what is expected of moderators (“mods”). It is not a legal document, but can be refined into one.

---

## 1. Purpose of Moderation

- Help keep SHK a safe, honest, and Christ-honoring space for sharing testimonies, prayer requests, and related content.
- Protect users from harassment, abuse, spam, and clearly inappropriate material.
- Support admins by handling everyday reports and edge cases, while leaving final authority and penalties to admins.

Moderators serve the community; they do not own or control it.

---

## 2. Your Role as a Moderator

As a mod you are a trusted user with extra tools. In simple terms, you:

- Review **reported content** (testimonies, prayer requests, and similar posts).
- Review content that is automatically **flagged by filters** (e.g., profanity or sensitive terms).
- Decide whether a post:
  - Clearly **follows the rules** and can be restored or left visible, or
  - **Probably breaks the rules** and should be escalated for an admin decision.

Your main job is to make careful, prayerful judgments about content and to communicate clearly in the tools you are given.

---

## 3. What Moderators Can Do

In the SHK tools, moderators can:

1. **See moderation queues**
   - View lists of reported posts.
   - View posts flagged by keyword filters.
   - See basic history for a post or account needed to make a decision.

2. **Handle reported content**
   - Mark a report as **“no violation”** when the content follows the rules.
     - This can restore a post that was temporarily hidden by reports.
   - Mark a post as **“violation recommended”** or **“escalate”** when you believe rules may be broken or when you are unsure.
   - Add short notes explaining why you made a decision.

3. **Work with sensitive content**
   - Note when content is sensitive but appropriate (for example, a testimony involving trauma or serious sin).
   - Recommend that the content remain with a sensitive warning, or be escalated if you are unsure.

4. **Communicate with admins**
   - Use notes and internal tools to flag difficult or repeated cases.
   - Ask questions when policies or edge cases are unclear.

---

## 4. What Moderators Cannot Do

To protect users and the integrity of the system, moderators **do not**:

- Ban or permanently suspend users.
- Directly apply or remove **strikes** on an account.
- Change a user’s **role** (user / mod / admin).
- Change a user’s **account status** (active / suspended / banned).
- Edit or view users’ **private drafts**, unless specifically allowed by admins in special circumstances.
- Edit global **settings** (like thresholds for auto-hide or strike rules).
- Use their role to target people they know personally or to push personal disagreements.

Final decisions about strikes, suspensions, and bans are made by admins, not mods.

---

## 5. How Moderation Works (High-Level)

### 5.1 Before a post goes live

- Some posts are checked against a list of blocked and sensitive words.
- If the post hits a **blocked term**, the author is asked to edit it or (in rare cases) submit for special admin review.
- If the post hits **review-only** or **sensitive** terms, it may be sent to a queue instead of going live immediately, depending on site settings.

### 5.2 Reports from users

- Any signed-in user can report a post or account.
- If a post receives reports from **multiple different users**, it may be automatically hidden and sent to the queue.
- Mods review these cases and either:
  - Confirm **no violation** and restore the post, or
  - Recommend a **violation / escalation** for admins.

### 5.3 Strikes, suspensions, and bans (admin responsibility)

- Admins apply **strikes** when they confirm that a user has broken the rules.
- Multiple strikes over time can lead to:
  - Temporary suspensions (read-only account, content hidden).
  - Long suspensions.
  - Permanent bans in the most serious or repeated cases.
- Before a permanent ban is finalized, admins review the case again.

Moderators help discover and recommend; admins decide and apply penalties.

---

## 6. Sensitive Content

Some testimonies may describe difficult topics (for example, abuse, self-harm, sexual sin, or trauma). The goal is to balance honesty with care and protection.

- Content may be marked as **sensitive** but still allowed when it is truthful and handled carefully.
- Users can choose to **hide sensitive content** in their settings.
- Moderators should:
  - Treat these stories with respect and compassion.
  - Avoid shaming users for what they have been through.
  - Focus decisions on whether content is harmful, unsafe, or violates guidelines—not on whether it is “messy.”
- When in doubt about sensitive content, escalate to admins rather than deciding alone.

---

## 7. Conduct and Confidentiality

As a moderator, you agree to the following principles:

1. **Confidentiality**
   - Do not share internal moderation details, logs, or private information outside the mod/admin team.
   - Do not discuss specific cases publicly unless an admin has made an official statement.

2. **Fairness and consistency**
   - Apply the same standards to friends, strangers, and people you disagree with.
   - Follow the written guidelines and any admin updates, even if your personal opinion is stricter or looser.

3. **Humility and accountability**
   - Be willing to have your decisions reviewed or corrected by admins.
   - Ask for help on unclear or difficult cases.
   - Step back from cases where you feel too emotionally involved to be objective.

4. **Spiritual posture**
   - Approach moderation as service, not control.
   - Aim for truth, grace, and safety together.

---

## 8. Agreement (Draft)

By serving as a moderator on SHK, you acknowledge that:

- You understand your **role**, **powers**, and **limits** as described in this document.
- You agree to follow the site’s **Terms & Conditions**, **Community Guidelines**, and any future updates provided by admins.
- You accept that admins may:
  - Review your moderation decisions.
  - Adjust your moderator permissions.
  - Remove your moderator role if necessary for the health and safety of the community.

This document can be updated over time as SHK grows and the moderation system matures.
