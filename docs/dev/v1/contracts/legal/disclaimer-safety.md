## 2. Faith, Theology, and Ministry Disclaimer

- **Not a church or denomination**  
  SHK is not a local church, denomination, or formal ecclesiastical body. It does not replace your involvement in a local church or fellowship.

- **No formal ordination or theological credentials claimed**  
  SHK is operated by an individual (and possibly a small team) who does not claim formal ordination or advanced theological accreditation. Any reflections, explanations, or applications shared through SHK are offered as personal perspectives and tools, not as official doctrinal rulings.

- **Not a teaching ministry in the formal sense**  
  SHK is not intended to serve as a formal teaching ministry or systematic theological program. Its primary aims are:
  - to provide access to Scripture and related tools,
  - to offer space for testimonies and encouragement,
  - to support personal application and community interaction.

- **Diversity of views in user content**  
  Testimonies, comments, and other User Content represent the views of their authors, not necessarily the views of SHK, its operators, or any particular church. SHK does not guarantee that all content is doctrinally precise or aligned with any specific tradition.

Users are encouraged to read Scripture for themselves, seek wisdom from their local church, and consult trusted Christian leaders when working through theological questions.

## 4. No Professional Advice

Content on SHK is meant to offer spiritual encouragement, personal reflections, and tools for engaging with Scripture. It is **not** professional advice.

SHK does **not** provide:

- professional counseling, psychotherapy, or mental health treatment;
- medical or psychiatric diagnosis, treatment, or prescriptions;
- legal, financial, or other regulated professional advice.

Nothing on the Site, including testimonies, reflections, or tools, should be taken as a substitute for:

- professional medical or mental-health care,
- professional legal or financial guidance,
- pastoral care and accountability in a local church.

You are responsible for your own decisions. When in doubt, seek appropriate professional and pastoral help.

## 5. No Emergency or Crisis Services

SHK is **not** an emergency service and is **not** monitored 24/7.

- If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, experiencing a medical or mental-health emergency, or at risk of harming themselves or others:
  - Call your local emergency number (such as 911 in the United States) immediately.
  - Contact local crisis or support services (for example, suicide prevention hotlines, domestic violence shelters, or other relevant organizations).
  - Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, pastor, or counselor in your area.

Do **not** wait for a response from SHK, moderators, or other users in any urgent or life-threatening situation.

## 6. Sensitive Content and Triggers

Because SHK hosts real testimonies and stories, you may encounter difficult topics, including:

- trauma, abuse, or exploitation;
- addiction and substance use;
- self-harm, suicidal thoughts, or severe mental health struggles;
- sexual sin or brokenness;
- other painful experiences and sins.

To help with this:

- Some content may be marked as **sensitive** and shown with a warning or hidden for users who choose not to view sensitive content.
- You are encouraged to:
  - pace yourself and step away if you feel overwhelmed,
  - avoid reading content you know may be triggering,
  - seek support offline (friends, family, church, counselors) when needed.

Even when moderated, the Site cannot guarantee that all content will be free of triggers or difficult material.

## 7. Limits of SHK’s Responses

When users share serious issues (such as self-harm, abuse, or severe distress), SHK’s capacity is limited:

- **Moderation, not clinical intervention**
  - Moderators and admins may prioritize reviewing potentially dangerous or highly sensitive content.
  - They may choose to hide, edit, or restrict content that appears harmful or unsafe to others.
  - They may offer general guidance or point toward seeking local, professional, or pastoral help.

- **No guarantee of response**
  - SHK and its moderators cannot guarantee that every post, message, or report will receive an individual response.
  - The Site may not be monitored in real time, and there may be delays in reviewing content or reports.

- **Scope of moderation decisions**
  - Moderation decisions are made for the safety and health of the community and may involve removing content, applying strikes, or limiting accounts as described in the Community Guidelines and Terms of Use.
  - These decisions are not clinical assessments, legal judgments, or formal pastoral rulings.
