## 1. What Is Seeking His Kingdom (SHK)?

**Seeking His Kingdom (SHK)** is a faith-based, non-denominational project created to help people:

- engage more deeply with **Scripture**,  
- share and read **testimonies** of what God has done, and  
- explore **tools and resources** that support a daily walk with Jesus.

At its core, SHK is a **hub for Scripture, testimony, and practical tools**—not a social network, not a church, and not a professional counseling service.

## 2. Vision and Heart Behind SHK

SHK began as a personal desire to:

- seek God’s kingdom first (inspired especially by Luke 12:31),  
- process Scripture, questions, and experiences in an honest way, and  
- create a space where others can do the same without pressure to “perform” spiritually.

The vision is to:

- encourage **daily pursuit of God and His Kingdom**,  
- provide simple, accessible tools that make it easier to open the Bible and talk about what God is doing, and  
- offer a place where real stories—victory, struggle, and everything in between—can point people back to Jesus.

## 3. What SHK Is and Is Not

**SHK _is_:**

- a personal faith-based project, currently stewarded by an individual (with occasional help from trusted family or friends),
- a space for Scripture, testimonies, and practical tools to apply faith,
- a platform for community interaction around stories and biblical resources, as features develop.

**SHK _is not_:**

- a local church, denomination, or formal ministry,
- a replacement for membership in a local church or pastoral oversight,
- a counseling, therapy, medical, legal, or financial service,
- an emergency or crisis-response service.

The person(s) behind SHK do **not** claim:

- formal ordination or pastoral office, or  
- formal advanced theological credentials.

Any explanations, reflections, or applications are offered as **personal perspectives and tools**, not as official doctrinal rulings. Users are encouraged to:

- read Scripture for themselves,  
- stay rooted in a local church, and  
- seek wise counsel from pastors, mentors, and appropriate professionals when needed.

For more detail on these limits and on crisis-related content, please see the **Disclaimers & Safety** page.
