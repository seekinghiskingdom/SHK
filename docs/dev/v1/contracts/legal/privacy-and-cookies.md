## 11. COOKIES & SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

### 11.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and tags.

### 11.2 How We Use Cookies

WE MAY USE COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES TO:

* REMEMBER YOUR SESSION AND KEEP YOU SIGNED IN;
* STORE BASIC PREFERENCES (E.G., THEME OR DISPLAY SETTINGS);
* COLLECT AGGREGATED ANALYTICS ABOUT HOW THE SITE IS USED (SUCH AS WHICH PAGES ARE VISITED AND FOR HOW LONG);
* IMPROVE PERFORMANCE, SECURITY, AND USER EXPERIENCE.

Some cookies are “session” cookies that expire when you close your browser; others are “persistent” and remain for a longer period or until deleted.

### 11.3 Types of Cookies

We may use:

* **Strictly necessary cookies** – required for basic site functionality (e.g., login, security);
* **Preference cookies** – remember choices or settings;
* **Analytics cookies** – help us understand how the Site is used;
* **Functionality or feature cookies** – support enhanced features.

IF WE INTRODUCE ADVERTISING OR TRACKING COOKIES IN THE FUTURE, WE WILL UPDATE THIS POLICY AND, WHERE REQUIRED, SEEK APPROPRIATE CONSENT.

### 11.4 Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to:

* View and delete cookies;
* Block cookies from specific sites;
* Block all cookies or certain categories of cookies.

You can usually find these options in your browser’s settings or help menu.

PLEASE NOTE THAT DISABLING SOME COOKIES MAY AFFECT YOUR ABILITY TO USE CERTAIN FEATURES OF THE SITE (SUCH AS STAYING LOGGED IN OR REMEMBERING PREFERENCES).
