For Parents & Leaders

THIS SECTION GATHERS A FEW STARTING IDEAS FOR ADULTS WHO ARE WALKING ALONGSIDE CHILDREN, WHETHER AT HOME, IN CHURCH, OR IN OTHER SETTINGS. -AI

  • USE RESOURCES AS A STARTING POINT FOR CONVERSATION, ASKING OPEN QUESTIONS LIKE “WHAT DID YOU NOTICE?” OR “WHAT QUESTIONS DO YOU HAVE?”. -AI
  • WHEN POSSIBLE, PAIR A RESOURCE WITH A SHORT BIBLE PASSAGE, A SIMPLE PRAYER, AND A CHANCE FOR CHILDREN TO SHARE IN THEIR OWN WORDS. -AI
  • CONSIDER THE AGE, PERSONALITY, AND ATTENTION SPAN OF EACH CHILD, AND FEEL FREE TO ADAPT OR SHORTEN ANY ACTIVITY AS NEEDED. -AI
  • CHECK ANY EXTERNAL LINKS OR MATERIALS BEFORE USING THEM WITH CHILDREN TO ENSURE THEY FIT YOUR FAMILY OR MINISTRY CONTEXT. -AI
