OVERVIEW -AI

THIS INITIATIVE GATHERS NEW AND GENTLY USED BIBLES SO THEY CAN BE GIVEN FREELY TO PEOPLE WHO WANT ONE BUT DO NOT HAVE EASY ACCESS OR RESOURCES. -AI

OUR GOAL IS TO KEEP THIS SIMPLE, PERSONAL, AND PRAYERFUL: HELPING CONNECT DONORS WHO HAVE EXTRA BIBLES WITH INDIVIDUALS, FAMILIES, AND MINISTRIES WHO ARE ASKING FOR ONE. -AI

FOR NOW, REQUESTS AND DONATIONS ARE COORDINATED MANUALLY THROUGH FORMS AND EMAIL, AND DETAILS LIKE SHIPPING, PICKUP, AND TRANSLATION OPTIONS ARE HANDLED CASE BY CASE. -AI

HOW THIS MINISTRY CURRENTLY WORKS -AI

LOGISTICS MAY CHANGE OVER TIME, BUT THE BASIC FLOW BELOW SHOWS HOW REQUESTS AND DONATIONS ARE USUALLY MATCHED AND PROCESSED. -AI

STEP 1: REQUESTS & DONATIONS RECEIVED -AI

INDIVIDUALS SUBMIT REQUESTS FOR BIBLES WHILE DONORS SHARE WHAT BIBLES THEY HAVE AVAILABLE, INCLUDING TRANSLATION AND CONDITION. -AI

STEP 2: MATCHING & FOLLOW-UP -AI

WE REVIEW REQUESTS, CHECK AVAILABLE BIBLES, AND REACH OUT BY EMAIL TO CONFIRM ADDRESSES, PREFERENCES, AND ANY SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS. -AI

STEP 3: SHIPPING OR PICKUP -AI

ONCE DETAILS ARE CONFIRMED, BIBLES ARE MAILED OR HANDED OFF AS PRACTICAL, WITH CARE TAKEN TO STEWARD DONATIONS AND RESOURCES WISELY. -AI

STEP 4: PRAYER & FOLLOW-UP ENCOURAGEMENT -AI

WHERE POSSIBLE, WE PRAY FOR EACH RECIPIENT AND MAY SHARE SIMPLE NEXT-STEP RESOURCES, SUCH AS READING GUIDES OR LOCAL CHURCH CONNECTION IDEAS. -AI

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS -AI

DOES IT COST ANYTHING TO RECEIVE A BIBLE? -AI
IN GENERAL THE GOAL IS TO PROVIDE BIBLES FREE OF CHARGE, THOUGH PRACTICAL LIMITS LIKE SHIPPING COSTS AND AVAILABLE DONATIONS MAY AFFECT WHAT IS POSSIBLE IN SOME CASES. -AI
CAN YOU SHIP BIBLES INTERNATIONALLY? -AI
INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING MAY BE LIMITED OR HANDLED DIFFERENTLY, SO EACH REQUEST WILL BE REVIEWED INDIVIDUALLY TO SEE WHAT OPTIONS ARE AVAILABLE. -AI
CAN I REQUEST A SPECIFIC TRANSLATION OR LANGUAGE? -AI
YOU CAN SHARE YOUR PREFERENCES ON THE REQUEST FORM, AND WE WILL DO OUR BEST TO MATCH THEM BASED ON WHAT HAS BEEN DONATED AND WHAT CAN BE OBTAINED. -AI
CAN CHURCHES OR MINISTRIES REQUEST MULTIPLE BIBLES? -AI
YES, GROUP OR MINISTRY REQUESTS CAN BE MADE, THOUGH FULFILLMENT WILL DEPEND ON DONATIONS, SHIPPING COSTS, AND OTHER PRACTICAL LIMITS AT THE TIME. -AI
CAN I GIVE FUNDS INSTEAD OF OR IN ADDITION TO BIBLES? -AI
OPTIONS FOR FINANCIAL SUPPORT MAY BE DEVELOPED LATER; FOR NOW THE FOCUS IS PRIMARILY ON PHYSICAL BIBLE DONATIONS AND DIRECT SHARING. -AI
