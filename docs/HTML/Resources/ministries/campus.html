WHAT IS CAMPUS MINISTRY? -AI

CAMPUS MINISTRY REFERS TO FOLLOWING JESUS AND SERVING OTHERS IN STUDENT ENVIRONMENTS, INCLUDING CLASSROOMS, RESIDENCE HALLS, CLUBS, LABS, AND INFORMAL SPACES ACROSS CAMPUS. -AI

IT OFTEN INVOLVES A MIX OF PERSONAL WITNESS, SMALL GROUPS, GATHERINGS, AND PARTNERSHIP WITH LOCAL CHURCHES OR CAMPUS MINISTRY ORGANIZATIONS. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, PRACTICAL IDEAS, AND CURATED LINKS CAN BE ADDED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT STUDENTS AND LEADERS IN CAMPUS CONTEXTS. -AI

LIFE AS A STUDENT -AI

CAMPUS LIFE OFTEN BRINGS NEW FREEDOMS, PRESSURES, AND QUESTIONS. MINISTRY THERE STARTS WITH LEARNING TO WALK WITH JESUS IN THE MIDDLE OF CLASSES, WORK, AND COMMUNITY. -AI

ROOTED IN CHRIST -AI

  • BUILD REGULAR RHYTHMS OF SCRIPTURE, PRAYER, AND COMMUNITY THAT FIT YOUR ACTUAL CLASS AND WORK SCHEDULE. -AI
  • LOOK FOR A HEALTHY LOCAL CHURCH AND COMMITTED BELIEVERS TO WALK WITH DURING YOUR TIME ON CAMPUS. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SIMPLE STARTER GUIDES FOR NEW STUDENTS ARRIVING ON CAMPUS. -AI

STEWARDING TIME & CALLING -AI

  • SEE YOUR STUDIES, RESEARCH, AND TRAINING AS PART OF HOW GOD IS PREPARING YOU TO SERVE HIM AND OTHERS. -AI
  • LEARN TO BALANCE CLASSWORK, REST, COMMUNITY, AND MINISTRY WITHOUT BURNOUT. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: CHECKLISTS OR ARTICLES ON MANAGING TIME AND EXPECTATIONS IN CAMPUS LIFE. -AI

INTEGRITY & WITNESS -AI

  • LIVE WITH HONESTY IN CLASSROOMS, EXAMS, LABS, AND GROUP PROJECTS AS PART OF YOUR WITNESS. -AI
  • HOW YOU HANDLE PRESSURE, FAILURE, AND SUCCESS CAN POINT OTHERS TO THE HOPE YOU HAVE IN CHRIST. -AI

GROUPS, GATHERINGS & COMMUNITY -AI

MANY CAMPUS MINISTRIES AND STUDENT GROUPS USE SMALL GROUPS, BIBLE STUDIES, AND LARGER GATHERINGS TO HELP STUDENTS CONNECT AND GROW. -AI

SMALL GROUPS & BIBLE STUDIES -AI

  • MEETING REGULARLY WITH A FEW STUDENTS TO READ SCRIPTURE AND PRAY CAN BE A FOUNDATION FOR CAMPUS MINISTRY. -AI
  • GROUPS CAN FORM AROUND DORMS, MAJORS, FRIEND GROUPS, OR EXISTING CAMPUS MINISTRY STRUCTURES. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SIMPLE STUDY OUTLINES AND DISCUSSION QUESTIONS THAT CAN BE ADAPTED LOCALLY. -AI

LARGE GATHERINGS & EVENTS -AI

  • WEEKLY MEETINGS, OUTREACH EVENTS, OR WORSHIP NIGHTS CAN HELP STUDENTS HEAR THE GOSPEL AND FIND COMMUNITY. -AI
  • KEEP THE PURPOSE CLEAR: INTRODUCING PEOPLE TO JESUS AND HELPING BELIEVERS GROW, NOT JUST FILLING CALENDARS. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: EVENT PLANNING BASICS AND FOLLOW-UP IDEAS AFTER GATHERINGS. -AI

CASUAL COMMUNITY SPACES -AI

  • MEALS, STUDY SESSIONS, AND INFORMAL HANGOUTS CAN BE NATURAL SPACES FOR FRIENDSHIP AND GOSPEL CONVERSATION. -AI
  • INCLUDE PEOPLE WHO ARE NEW, LONELY, OR ON THE EDGES OF COMMUNITY, NOT ONLY CLOSE FRIENDS. -AI

WITNESS & EVANGELISM ON CAMPUS -AI

CAMPUS ENVIRONMENTS OFTEN BRING PEOPLE FROM MANY BACKGROUNDS TOGETHER. THIS CAN CREATE UNIQUE OPPORTUNITIES TO SHARE THE HOPE OF THE GOSPEL. -AI

RELATIONAL WITNESS -AI

  • LISTEN WELL TO CLASSMATES AND FRIENDS, ASK GOOD QUESTIONS, AND SHARE HOW JESUS MATTERS IN YOUR OWN LIFE. -AI
  • BE PATIENT; FRIENDSHIPS AND TRUST OFTEN GROW OVER TIME, NOT IN ONE CONVERSATION. -AI

GOSPEL CONVERSATIONS -AI

  • LOOK FOR NATURAL MOMENTS TO EXPLAIN THE CORE OF THE GOSPEL CLEARLY AND GENTLY. -AI
  • IT IS OKAY TO SAY “I DO NOT KNOW” AND THEN SEEK ANSWERS TOGETHER. -AI

SERVICE & JUSTICE -AI

  • PARTICIPATING IN ACTS OF SERVICE OR JUSTICE CAN OPEN DOORS FOR GOSPEL-ROOTED CONVERSATIONS. -AI
  • KEEP THE FOCUS ON LOVING NEIGHBORS AND POINTING TO CHRIST, NOT ONLY ON PROJECTS OR CAUSES. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SIMPLE EVANGELISM TOOLS, QUESTION LISTS, AND LINKS TO TRAINING MATERIALS FOR CAMPUS CONTEXTS. -AI

LOCAL CHURCH & CAMPUS MINISTRY PARTNERS -AI

CAMPUS MINISTRY IS HEALTHIER WHEN IT IS CONNECTED TO LOCAL CHURCHES AND, WHERE APPROPRIATE, TO CAMPUS MINISTRY ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE SUPPORT AND ACCOUNTABILITY. -AI

  • LOOK FOR A LOCAL CHURCH THAT TEACHES SCRIPTURE CLEARLY AND CARES WELL FOR PEOPLE. -AI
  • WHERE CAMPUS MINISTRIES EXIST, CONSIDER CONNECTING WITH THEM RATHER THAN STARTING ALONE. -AI
  • SEEK MENTORS AND LEADERS WHO CAN WALK WITH YOU THROUGH DECISIONS, STRUGGLES, AND OPPORTUNITIES ON CAMPUS. -AI
