WHAT ARE MISSIONS & SENDING? -AI

MISSIONS REFERS TO INTENTIONALLY SHARING THE GOSPEL AND SERVING OTHERS BEYOND YOUR USUAL CONTEXT, OFTEN ACROSS CULTURAL, LINGUISTIC, OR GEOGRAPHIC BOUNDARIES. -AI

SENDING INVOLVES SUPPORTING THOSE WHO GO—THROUGH PRAYER, ENCOURAGEMENT, PRACTICAL CARE, AND FINANCIAL HELP—AS PART OF ONE BODY WORKING TOGETHER. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, PRAYER HELPS, AND RESOURCE LINKS CAN BE ADDED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT BOTH GOERS AND SENDERS. -AI

WAYS TO PARTICIPATE IN MISSIONS -AI

NOT EVERYONE IS CALLED TO THE SAME ROLE, BUT EVERY BELIEVER CAN PARTICIPATE IN GOD’S MISSION IN SOME WAY. -AI

PRAYING -AI

  • PRAY REGULARLY FOR PEOPLE, PLACES, AND MINISTRIES, EVEN IF YOU NEVER TRAVEL THERE YOURSELF. -AI
  • USE SIMPLE PRAYER LISTS, MAPS, OR UPDATES FROM WORKERS AS HELPFUL REMINDERS. -AI

GOING (SHORT-TERM OR LONGER) -AI

  • SHORT-TERM TRIPS, INTERNSHIPS, OR LONGER-TERM ASSIGNMENTS CAN SUPPORT EXISTING WORK IN OTHER PLACES. -AI
  • HEALTHY GOING USUALLY CONNECTS TO LOCAL CHURCHES OR MATURE SENDING MINISTRIES, NOT ISOLATED EFFORTS. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: CHECKLISTS AND QUESTIONS TO ASK BEFORE JOINING OR PLANNING A TRIP. -AI

GIVING & PRACTICAL SUPPORT -AI

  • FINANCIAL SUPPORT, LOGISTICAL HELP, AND PROFESSIONAL SKILLS CAN ALL SERVE THOSE WHO GO. -AI
  • STEADY, THOUGHTFUL SUPPORT OVER TIME CAN BE AS CRUCIAL AS THE WORK ON THE FIELD. -AI

WELCOMING & SENDING FROM HOME -AI

  • HOSTING, ENCOURAGING, AND WELCOMING WORKERS WHEN THEY VISIT OR RETURN IS A REAL MINISTRY. -AI
  • YOU CAN ALSO WELCOME INTERNATIONAL NEIGHBORS OR STUDENTS WHERE YOU LIVE AS PART OF MISSIONS. -AI

ADVOCATING & MOBILIZING -AI

  • SHARING STORIES, LEADING PRAYER TIMES, OR ORGANIZING SUPPORT CAN HELP OTHERS JOIN IN GOD’S WORK. -AI
  • EVEN SIMPLE COMMUNICATION, UPDATES, OR SMALL GROUP FOCUS TIMES CAN BE PART OF MOBILIZING. -AI

STAYING & SERVING LOCALLY -AI

  • STAYING WHERE YOU ARE, FAITHFULLY SERVING IN LOCAL CHURCHES, WORKPLACES, AND NEIGHBORHOODS, IS ALSO PART OF GOD’S GLOBAL PURPOSES. -AI
  • LOCAL FAITHFULNESS AND GLOBAL CONCERN BELONG TOGETHER, NOT IN COMPETITION. -AI

LOCAL & GLOBAL DIMENSIONS OF MISSIONS -AI

MISSIONS IS BOTH “TO THE ENDS OF THE EARTH” AND “RIGHT WHERE YOU LIVE,” OFTEN INTERWEAVING LOCAL AND GLOBAL CONNECTIONS. -AI

LOCAL EXPRESSIONS -AI

  • LOVING NEIGHBORS, SERVING THOSE IN NEED, AND SHARING THE GOSPEL LOCALLY ARE PART OF THE SAME STORY. -AI
  • LOCAL CHURCHES CAN HAVE A HEART FOR THEIR CITY WHILE ALSO CARING ABOUT THE WORLD. -AI

GLOBAL CONNECTIONS -AI

  • PARTNERING WITH WORKERS OR CHURCHES IN OTHER COUNTRIES CAN HELP SHARE RESOURCES AND ENCOURAGEMENT BOTH WAYS. -AI
  • GLOBAL MISSIONS OFTEN INVOLVE TEAMWORK ACROSS CULTURES, GIFTS, AND BACKGROUNDS. -AI

NEAR & FAR TOGETHER -AI

  • PRAYING FOR AND SUPPORTING GLOBAL WORK CAN STRENGTHEN YOUR LOCAL CHURCH’S HEART FOR PEOPLE NEARBY. -AI
  • LOCAL OBEDIENCE AND GLOBAL VISION CAN GROW SIDE BY SIDE OVER TIME. -AI

DISCERNMENT & PREPARATION -AI

DECIDING HOW TO ENGAGE IN MISSIONS—ESPECIALLY GOING CROSS-CULTURALLY FOR LONGER PERIODS—USUALLY REQUIRES CAREFUL PRAYER, COUNSEL, AND PREPARATION. -AI

PRAYER & MOTIVES -AI

  • SEEK GOD ABOUT WHY YOU WANT TO GO OR SERVE, ASKING HIM TO SHAPE YOUR MOTIVES AROUND LOVE AND OBEDIENCE. -AI
  • BE HONEST ABOUT FEARS, HOPES, AND EXPECTATIONS, BRINGING THEM INTO THE LIGHT WITH TRUSTED BELIEVERS. -AI

LOCAL FAITHFULNESS FIRST -AI

  • OFTEN, HEALTHY MISSIONS INVOLVEMENT GROWS OUT OF FAITHFULNESS IN LOCAL CHURCH, WORK, AND RELATIONSHIPS. -AI
  • BEING TRUSTWORTHY IN SMALL THINGS CAN BE PART OF PREPARATION FOR OTHER OPPORTUNITIES. -AI

TRAINING & TEAM -AI

  • LEARNING ABOUT SCRIPTURE, CULTURE, LANGUAGE, AND PRACTICAL SKILLS HELPS PREPARE YOU TO SERVE WELL. -AI
  • BEING SENT BY A LOCAL CHURCH OR MATURE TEAM PROVIDES SUPPORT, ACCOUNTABILITY, AND WISDOM. -AI

CARE, PARTNERSHIP & LONG-TERM HEALTH -AI

LONG-TERM MISSIONS INVOLVEMENT REQUIRES ONGOING CARE, RELATIONSHIPS, AND HEALTHY RHYTHMS FOR BOTH GOERS AND SENDERS. -AI

  • REGULAR CHECK-INS, PRAYER, AND ENCOURAGEMENT HELP WORKERS PERSEVERE AND STAY ROOTED IN COMMUNITY. -AI
  • REST, SABBATH, AND SEASONS OF RENEWAL ARE IMPORTANT FOR LONG-TERM STABILITY AND FAITHFULNESS. -AI
  • HONEST CONVERSATIONS ABOUT STRUGGLES, DOUBTS, AND CHANGES CAN PREVENT HIDDEN EXHAUSTION OR ISOLATION. -AI
