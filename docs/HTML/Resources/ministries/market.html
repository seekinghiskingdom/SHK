WHAT IS WORKPLACE & MARKETPLACE MINISTRY? -AI

WORKPLACE & MARKETPLACE MINISTRY IS ABOUT SERVING CHRIST IN JOBS, PROFESSIONS, AND EVERYDAY ECONOMIC LIFE, WHETHER IN OFFICES, LABS, SHOPS, FIELDS, HOMES, OR ONLINE. -AI

IT COVERS BOTH FORMAL OPPORTUNITIES (SUCH AS WORKPLACE BIBLE STUDIES OR PRAYER GROUPS) AND INFORMAL WAYS OF LOVING NEIGHBORS, DOING EXCELLENT WORK, AND LIVING WITH INTEGRITY. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, CURATED LINKS, AND TESTIMONY-STYLE RESOURCES CAN BE GATHERED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT BELIEVERS IN THEIR WORK CONTEXTS. -AI

DIFFERENT KINDS OF CALLINGS -AI

  • GOD MAY CALL SOME TO FORMAL MINISTRY ROLES, BUT HE ALSO USES TEACHERS, ENGINEERS, PARENTS, ARTISTS, CAREGIVERS, AND MANY OTHERS. -AI
  • CALLING CAN INVOLVE GIFTS, OPPORTUNITIES, NEEDS AROUND YOU, AND WISE COUNSEL FROM OTHERS. -AI

SEASONS OF WORK -AI

  • SEASONS OF STUDY, JOB SEARCHING, UNPAID WORK, OR TRANSITION ARE ALSO PART OF YOUR STORY BEFORE GOD. -AI
  • GOD’S FAITHFULNESS DOES NOT DEPEND ON STABILITY OR STATUS IN YOUR CAREER. -AI

INTEGRITY, ETHICS & CHARACTER AT WORK -AI

WORKPLACES OFTEN PRESENT ETHICAL QUESTIONS AND PRESSURES. FOLLOWING JESUS SHAPES HOW WE NAVIGATE THESE REALITIES. -AI

HONESTY & TRANSPARENCY -AI

  • AVOID DISHONESTY IN REPORTING, COMMUNICATION, AND RELATIONSHIPS, EVEN WHEN IT SEEMS SMALL OR EXPECTED. -AI
  • WHEN POSSIBLE, SPEAK TRUTHFULLY AND GENTLY, SEEKING PEACE WITHOUT HIDING REALITY. -AI

JUSTICE & FAIRNESS -AI

  • CARE ABOUT HOW DECISIONS AFFECT COWORKERS, CUSTOMERS, AND COMMUNITIES, ESPECIALLY THE VULNERABLE. -AI
  • WHERE YOU HAVE INFLUENCE, USE IT TO SUPPORT FAIR TREATMENT AND HONOR OTHERS’ DIGNITY. -AI

BOUNDARIES & COMPROMISE -AI

  • SOME SITUATIONS MAY REQUIRE SAYING NO OR SEEKING COUNSEL TO AVOID COMPROMISING YOUR CONSCIENCE. -AI
  • SEEK WISE ADVICE FROM TRUSTED BELIEVERS WHEN FACING HARD ETHICAL DECISIONS AT WORK. -AI

PRESENCE, SERVICE & WITNESS IN THE WORKPLACE -AI

WITNESS AT WORK IS OFTEN SLOW, RELATIONAL, AND GROUNDED IN HOW YOU TREAT PEOPLE DAY AFTER DAY. -AI

RELATIONAL PRESENCE -AI

  • LISTEN TO COWORKERS, LEARN THEIR STORIES, AND CARE ABOUT THEM AS PEOPLE, NOT ONLY AS COLLABORATORS. -AI
  • CONSISTENT KINDNESS AND PATIENCE CAN OPEN DOORS FOR DEEPER CONVERSATIONS OVER TIME. -AI

GOSPEL CONVERSATIONS -AI

  • LOOK FOR NATURAL MOMENTS TO SHARE WHY JESUS MATTERS TO YOU, WITHOUT PRESSURE OR ARGUMENT. -AI
  • RESPECT WORKPLACE POLICIES AND OTHERS’ FREEDOM WHILE STILL BEING HONEST ABOUT YOUR HOPE. -AI

SERVICE & EXCELLENCE -AI

  • DOING WORK WELL, SUPPORTING YOUR TEAM, AND SERVING OTHERS QUIETLY CAN REFLECT GOD’S CHARACTER. -AI
  • EVEN WHEN YOUR ROLE FEELS SMALL, FAITHFULNESS THERE IS NOT SMALL TO GOD. -AI

PRESSURE, LIMITS & HEALTH AT WORK -AI

WORK CAN BRING STRESS, LONG HOURS, AND COMPLEX EXPECTATIONS. LEARNING TO HONOR GOD IN HOW YOU HANDLE PRESSURE IS PART OF WORKPLACE MINISTRY. -AI

  • RECOGNIZE YOUR LIMITS AND SEEK RHYTHMS OF REST, SLEEP, AND HEALTHY BOUNDARIES WHERE POSSIBLE. -AI
  • WHEN WORK BECOMES DAMAGING TO YOUR HEALTH OR CONSCIENCE, SEEK COUNSEL AND PRAYER ABOUT NEXT STEPS. -AI
  • REMEMBER THAT YOUR IDENTITY IS IN CHRIST, NOT IN YOUR PERFORMANCE OR JOB TITLE. -AI
