WHAT IS PERSONAL & HOME MINISTRY? -AI

PERSONAL & HOME MINISTRY MEANS FOLLOWING JESUS IN THE ORDINARY MOMENTS OF LIFE: PRAYER, SCRIPTURE, WORK, REST, AND RELATIONSHIPS INSIDE YOUR HOUSEHOLD AND CLOSE CIRCLES. -AI

THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM FORMAL ROLES OR PROGRAMS; IT IS ABOUT WHO YOU ARE IN CHRIST AND HOW HIS LIFE SHAPES YOUR HABITS, WORDS, AND CHOICES WHERE YOU ALREADY LIVE. -AI

THIS PAGE IS A STARTING PLACE FOR SIMPLE GUIDES, IDEAS, AND EXTERNAL RESOURCES THAT CAN SUPPORT YOUR PERSONAL WALK AND WITNESS, EVEN BEFORE YOU ARE INVOLVED IN ORGANIZED MINISTRY. -AI

WITNESS IN THE HOME -AI

YOUR HOME IS OFTEN THE FIRST PLACE WHERE YOUR FAITH IS SEEN UP CLOSE—BY FAMILY, ROOMMATES, OR CLOSE FRIENDS. -AI

ORDINARY FAITHFULNESS -AI

  • SMALL ACTS OF PATIENCE, FORGIVENESS, SERVICE, AND TRUTH TELL A STORY ABOUT GOD’S CHARACTER. -AI
  • APOLOGIZING WHEN YOU FAIL CAN OFTEN SPEAK LOUDER THAN ALWAYS APPEARING STRONG. -AI

GENTLE CONVERSATIONS -AI

  • LOOK FOR NATURAL MOMENTS TO SHARE WHY JESUS MATTERS TO YOU, WITHOUT FORCING ARGUMENTS. -AI
  • ASK QUESTIONS, LISTEN WELL, AND BE READY TO ADMIT WHEN YOU DO NOT KNOW AN ANSWER. -AI

HOSPITALITY & PRESENCE -AI

  • WELCOMING OTHERS INTO YOUR SPACE, EVEN IN SIMPLE WAYS, CAN BECOME A CONTEXT FOR GOSPEL CONVERSATION. -AI
  • HOSPITALITY DOES NOT REQUIRE A PERFECT HOME, ONLY A WILLING HEART AND OPEN HANDS. -AI

  • WHEN POSSIBLE, SEEK WISE COUNSEL FROM MATURE BELIEVERS OR LOCAL LEADERS WHO KNOW YOUR SITUATION. -AI
  • TAKE CARE WITH SAFETY, WISDOM, AND RESPECT, ESPECIALLY WHERE FAITH MAY LEAD TO RELATIONAL OR PRACTICAL RISK. -AI
  • REMEMBER THAT GOD SEES YOUR CONTEXT CLEARLY, EVEN WHEN OTHERS DO NOT. -AI
