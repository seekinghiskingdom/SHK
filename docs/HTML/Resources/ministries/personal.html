WHAT IS PERSONAL & HOME MINISTRY? -AI

PERSONAL & HOME MINISTRY REFERS TO HOW YOU FOLLOW JESUS IN YOUR OWN LIFE AND IN THE PLACES YOU LIVE—YOUR HABITS, RELATIONSHIPS, AND DAILY RHYTHMS WHEN NO ONE ELSE IS WATCHING. -AI

IT INCLUDES PRAYER, SCRIPTURE, REPENTANCE, REST, AND THE WAY YOU TREAT PEOPLE IN YOUR HOUSEHOLD, BUILDING A QUIET FOUNDATION FOR OTHER MINISTRY CONTEXTS LIKE CHURCH, WORK, CAMPUS, AND MISSIONS. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, HABIT IDEAS, AND RESOURCE LINKS CAN BE GATHERED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT INDIVIDUALS AND HOUSEHOLDS. -AI

PERSONAL WALK WITH GOD -AI

PERSONAL MINISTRY BEGINS WITH YOUR OWN RELATIONSHIP WITH GOD—KNOWING HIM, TRUSTING HIM, AND RETURNING TO HIM IN BOTH JOY AND STRUGGLE. -AI

SCRIPTURE & PRAYER -AI

  • SET ASIDE REGULAR TIMES, EVEN SHORT ONES, FOR READING SCRIPTURE AND PRAYING HONESTLY. -AI
  • USE SIMPLE PATTERNS, SUCH AS A PASSAGE PLUS A SHORT RESPONSE PRAYER OR JOURNAL NOTE. -AI

REPENTANCE & GROWTH -AI

  • BRING SIN, WEAKNESS, AND FEARS INTO THE LIGHT BEFORE GOD INSTEAD OF HIDING THEM. -AI
  • ASK FOR HELP TO GROW IN SPECIFIC AREAS, RATHER THAN ONLY GENERAL PRAYERS TO “DO BETTER.” -AI

REST & DEPENDENCE -AI

  • LEARN TO RECEIVE REST AND LIMITS AS PART OF TRUSTING GOD, NOT AS FAILURE. -AI
  • SEASONS OF WEAKNESS CAN STILL BE FRUITFUL WHEN YOU LEAN ON GOD AND OTHERS. -AI

HOME & HOUSEHOLD CONTEXTS -AI

HOME CAN BE A PLACE OF FORMAL FAMILY MINISTRY OR SIMPLY A SPACE YOU SHARE WITH ROOMMATES OR OTHERS. PERSONAL MINISTRY THERE OFTEN LOOKS LIKE EVERYDAY FAITHFULNESS. -AI

FAMILY LIFE -AI

  • FOR THOSE WITH FAMILIES, SIMPLE PRACTICES LIKE PRAYING TOGETHER OR READING SMALL PORTIONS OF SCRIPTURE CAN BE MEANINGFUL. -AI
  • FAMILY MINISTRY HAS ITS OWN PAGE, BUT THIS SECTION HIGHLIGHTS PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY IN THE MIDST OF FAMILY LIFE. -AI

ROOMMATES & SHARED SPACES -AI

  • HOW YOU HANDLE CHORES, FINANCES, CONFLICT, AND SCHEDULES CAN BE PART OF YOUR WITNESS AT HOME. -AI
  • RESPECT, HONESTY, AND PATIENCE CAN MAKE SHARED LIVING SPACES SAFER AND MORE STABLE. -AI

ORDINARY RHYTHMS -AI

  • COOKING, CLEANING, RESTING, AND HOSTING OTHERS CAN BE RECEIVED AS PART OF YOUR LIFE WITH GOD. -AI
  • YOU CAN INTENTIONALLY INVITE GOD INTO DAILY TASKS THROUGH PRAYER AND THANKFULNESS. -AI

NEIGHBORS & EVERYDAY RELATIONSHIPS -AI

PERSONAL MINISTRY OFTEN SHOWS UP IN SMALL ACTS OF LOVE TOWARD NEIGHBORS, FRIENDS, AND PEOPLE YOU REGULARLY SEE. -AI

BEING PRESENT -AI

  • LEARN YOUR NEIGHBORS’ NAMES, STORIES, AND NEEDS WHERE APPROPRIATE. -AI
  • BE OPEN TO SMALL CONVERSATIONS OR PRACTICAL HELP AS OPPORTUNITIES ARISE. -AI

HOSPITALITY -AI

  • HOSPITALITY DOES NOT HAVE TO BE ELABORATE; SIMPLE MEALS OR GENTLE WELCOME CAN MATTER A LOT. -AI
  • INVITING OTHERS INTO YOUR HOME OR RHYTHMS CAN OPEN SPACE FOR GOSPEL-ROOTED FRIENDSHIP. -AI

GOSPEL CONVERSATIONS -AI

  • SHARE HOW JESUS MATTERS IN YOUR LIFE WHEN OPPORTUNITIES COME, WITHOUT PRESSURE OR PRETENSE. -AI
  • LISTEN WELL, ASK QUESTIONS, AND BE WILLING TO SAY “I DO NOT KNOW” WHEN NEEDED. -AI

SEASONS, STRUGGLES & CHANGE -AI

PERSONAL & HOME MINISTRY WILL LOOK DIFFERENT IN DIFFERENT SEASONS—INCLUDING TIMES OF ILLNESS, BUSYNESS, TRANSITION, OR SUFFERING. -AI

HEAVY OR LIMITING SEASONS -AI

  • DURING DIFFICULT SEASONS, YOUR RHYTHMS MAY NEED TO BECOME SIMPLER AND MORE GENTLE. -AI
  • GOD IS NOT SURPRISED BY YOUR LIMITS; SMALL ACTS OF TRUST CAN STILL BE DEEPLY MEANINGFUL. -AI

TRANSITIONS & CHANGE -AI

  • MOVES, NEW JOBS, OR FAMILY CHANGES OFTEN REQUIRE REBUILDING RHYTHMS OF SCRIPTURE, PRAYER, AND COMMUNITY. -AI
  • ALLOW YOURSELF TIME TO RE-ESTABLISH PRACTICES RATHER THAN EXPECTING INSTANT STABILITY. -AI

ASKING FOR HELP -AI

  • REACHING OUT TO TRUSTED FRIENDS, LEADERS, OR COUNSELORS CAN BE PART OF PERSONAL MINISTRY, NOT A FAILURE OF IT. -AI
  • YOU DO NOT HAVE TO CARRY STRUGGLES ALONE; THE BODY OF CHRIST IS MEANT TO SHARE BURDENS. -AI
