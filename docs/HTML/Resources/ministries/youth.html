WHAT IS YOUTH & STUDENT MINISTRY? -AI

YOUTH & STUDENT MINISTRY REFERS TO SERVING TEENS AND STUDENTS (OFTEN MIDDLE SCHOOL, HIGH SCHOOL, AND EARLY COLLEGE) THROUGH RELATIONSHIPS, GATHERINGS, AND DISCIPLESHIP. -AI

THIS PAGE IS PRIMARILY AIMED AT THOSE WHO SERVE YOUNG PEOPLE—LEADERS, VOLUNTEERS, PARENTS, AND MENTORS—WHILE ALSO PROVIDING LINKS THAT STUDENTS THEMSELVES MAY FIND HELPFUL. -AI

CAMPUS MINISTRY DESERVES ITS OWN SPACE, SO COLLEGE-FOCUSED CONTENT ON UNIVERSITY CAMPUSES IS HIGHLIGHTED THERE, WHILE THIS PAGE REMAINS BROAD ENOUGH FOR YOUTH GROUPS, STUDENT MINISTRY, AND MIXED SETTINGS. -AI

GOALS & FOCUS OF YOUTH MINISTRY -AI

WHILE EVERY CONTEXT IS DIFFERENT, MANY HEALTHY YOUTH MINISTRIES SHARE A FEW CORE GOALS. -AI

KNOWING JESUS -AI

  • HELP STUDENTS MEET, TRUST, AND FOLLOW JESUS, NOT JUST ATTEND EVENTS OR PROGRAMS. -AI
  • KEEP SCRIPTURE AND THE GOSPEL CENTRAL IN TEACHING, DISCUSSION, AND RELATIONSHIPS. -AI

GROWING IN COMMUNITY -AI

  • CREATE ENVIRONMENTS WHERE STUDENTS CAN BE KNOWN, LOVED, AND ENCOURAGED TOWARD CHRIST. -AI
  • MODEL GRACE, TRUTH, AND HEALTHY RELATIONSHIPS AMONG LEADERS AND STUDENTS. -AI

LEARNING TO SERVE & SHARE -AI

  • GIVE STUDENTS AGE-APPROPRIATE WAYS TO SERVE OTHERS AND SHARE THEIR FAITH. -AI
  • HELP THEM SEE THEIR SCHOOL, FAMILY, TEAMS, AND FRIEND GROUPS AS PLACES GOD CAN USE THEM. -AI

GATHERINGS, GROUPS & EVENTS -AI

MANY YOUTH MINISTRIES USE A MIX OF LARGE GATHERINGS, SMALL GROUPS, AND SPECIAL EVENTS TO HELP STUDENTS CONNECT, LEARN, AND GROW. -AI

REGULAR GATHERINGS -AI

  • WEEKLY OR BIWEEKLY MEETINGS CAN INCLUDE TEACHING, WORSHIP, GAMES, AND DISCUSSION. -AI
  • KEEP THE PURPOSE CLEAR: HELP STUDENTS ENCOUNTER GOD AND HIS WORD, NOT JUST ENTERTAINMENT. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SAMPLE GATHERING STRUCTURES AND SIMPLE PLANNING CHECKLISTS. -AI

SMALL GROUPS -AI

  • SMALL GROUPS GIVE SPACE FOR STUDENTS TO ASK QUESTIONS, SHARE HONESTLY, AND APPLY SCRIPTURE. -AI
  • TRAIN LEADERS TO LISTEN WELL, HANDLE QUESTIONS HUMBLY, AND POINT CONSISTENTLY TO CHRIST. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SIMPLE SMALL-GROUP GUIDES AND QUESTION SETS THAT CAN BE ADAPTED LOCALLY. -AI

CAMPS, RETREATS & SPECIAL EVENTS -AI

  • RETREATS, CAMPS, AND SPECIAL EVENTS CAN GIVE EXTENDED TIME FOR TEACHING, COMMUNITY, AND DECISION-MAKING. -AI
  • USE THEM TO SUPPORT ONGOING RELATIONSHIPS, NOT AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR REGULAR DISCIPLESHIP. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: PLANNING CONSIDERATIONS AND FOLLOW-UP IDEAS AFTER EVENTS. -AI

DISCIPLESHIP & MENTORING -AI

BEYOND PROGRAMS, YOUTH MINISTRY THRIVES WHEN LEADERS WALK WITH STUDENTS OVER TIME, HELPING THEM GROW PERSONALLY IN THEIR FAITH. -AI

ONE-ON-ONE & SMALL DISCIPLESHIP GROUPS -AI

  • MEET REGULARLY WITH A FEW STUDENTS TO READ SCRIPTURE, PRAY, AND TALK ABOUT LIFE. -AI
  • KEEP EXPECTATIONS CLEAR AND APPROPRIATE, WITH HEALTHY BOUNDARIES AND ACCOUNTABILITY. -AI

HELPING STUDENTS OWN THEIR FAITH -AI

  • INVITE STUDENTS TO ASK REAL QUESTIONS AND WRESTLE WITH DOUBTS IN THE LIGHT, NOT IN HIDING. -AI
  • POINT THEM TO SCRIPTURE, PRAYER, WISE COUNSEL, AND LOCAL CHURCH INVOLVEMENT AS FOUNDATIONS. -AI

PREPARING FOR TRANSITIONS -AI

  • HELP STUDENTS PREPARE FOR LIFE TRANSITIONS (SUCH AS GRADUATION OR MOVING AWAY) WITH PRACTICAL STEPS FOR STAYING ROOTED IN JESUS. -AI
  • ENCOURAGE THEM TO LOOK FOR A HEALTHY CHURCH AND CHRIST-CENTERED COMMUNITY IN NEW PLACES. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SIMPLE DISCIPLESHIP OUTLINES, CHECKLISTS, AND LINKS TO ARTICLES OR STUDIES FOR YOUNG BELIEVERS. -AI

PARTNERING WITH PARENTS & THE LOCAL CHURCH -AI

HEALTHY YOUTH MINISTRY WORKS ALONGSIDE FAMILIES AND THE WIDER CHURCH, NOT SEPARATE FROM THEM. -AI

SUPPORTING PARENTS -AI

  • SEE PARENTS AND GUARDIANS AS PRIMARY DISCIPLERS IN STUDENTS’ LIVES WHENEVER POSSIBLE. -AI
  • COMMUNICATE CLEARLY, SHARE WHAT STUDENTS ARE LEARNING, AND OFFER SIMPLE RESOURCES THEY CAN USE AT HOME. -AI

INTEGRATION WITH CHURCH LIFE -AI

  • HELP STUDENTS PARTICIPATE IN THE BROADER CHURCH, NOT ONLY IN AGE-SEGMENTED SETTINGS. -AI
  • ENCOURAGE INTERGENERATIONAL RELATIONSHIPS AND SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES. -AI

SAFETY, POLICIES & CARE -AI

  • TAKE SAFETY, APPROPRIATE BOUNDARIES, AND CARE POLICIES SERIOUSLY IN ALL YOUTH SETTINGS. -AI
  • WHEN POSSIBLE, FOLLOW LOCAL CHURCH OR ORGANIZATIONAL GUIDELINES AND TRAIN LEADERS WELL. -AI
