WHAT IS INTERNATIONAL & CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY? -AI

INTERNATIONAL & CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY REFERS TO SERVING CHRIST AND OTHERS ACROSS LINES OF CULTURE, LANGUAGE, NATIONALITY, OR ETHNIC BACKGROUND, WHETHER IN YOUR OWN CITY OR IN OTHER PARTS OF THE WORLD. -AI

THIS CAN INCLUDE FRIENDSHIPS WITH INTERNATIONAL NEIGHBORS OR STUDENTS, PARTICIPATION IN SHORT-TERM OR LONG-TERM SERVICE, SUPPORTING THOSE WHO ARE SENT, AND CARING FOR PEOPLE FROM MANY NATIONS IN PRACTICAL WAYS. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, STORIES, AND LINKS CAN BE ADDED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT FAITHFUL, HUMBLE, AND WISE CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY. -AI

HEART & POSTURE IN CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY -AI

CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY IS NOT ONLY ABOUT TRAVEL OR PROJECTS; IT IS ABOUT LEARNING TO LOVE PEOPLE AS NEIGHBORS, LISTENING WELL, AND HONORING THEIR DIGNITY WHILE POINTING TO CHRIST. -AI

HUMILITY & LEARNING -AI

  • ENTER OTHER CULTURES AND CONTEXTS AS A LEARNER, NOT AS AN EXPERT OR FIXER. -AI
  • BE READY TO LISTEN, ASK QUESTIONS, AND RECEIVE FROM OTHERS, NOT ONLY TO GIVE. -AI

RESPECT & DIGNITY -AI

  • SEE EACH PERSON AS MADE IN GOD’S IMAGE, WITH THEIR OWN STORY, HISTORY, AND CONTEXT. -AI
  • AVOID TREATING PEOPLE OR PLACES AS PROJECTS; FOCUS ON REAL RELATIONSHIPS. -AI

PRAYERFUL DEPENDENCE -AI

  • PRAY FOR WISDOM, LOVE, AND PATIENCE AS YOU ENGAGE ACROSS CULTURES. -AI
  • REMEMBER THAT GOD IS ALREADY AT WORK LONG BEFORE YOU ARRIVE AND LONG AFTER YOU LEAVE. -AI

EVERYDAY CROSS-CULTURAL OPPORTUNITIES -AI

MANY PEOPLE ENCOUNTER INTERNATIONAL OR CROSS-CULTURAL NEIGHBORS WITHOUT LEAVING THEIR CITY, SCHOOL, CAMPUS, OR WORKPLACE. -AI

LOCAL NEIGHBORS & COMMUNITY -AI

  • LOOK FOR INTERNATIONAL NEIGHBORS, REFUGEES, OR IMMIGRANT COMMUNITIES NEARBY AND WELCOME THEM WITH PRACTICAL CARE. -AI
  • SHARING MEALS, HELPING WITH PRACTICAL NEEDS, OR LEARNING BASIC PHRASES IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE CAN BUILD TRUST. -AI

INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS & VISITORS -AI

  • CAMPUSES AND CITIES OFTEN HOST STUDENTS, SCHOLARS, OR WORKERS FROM MANY COUNTRIES WHO MAY BE FAR FROM FAMILY. -AI
  • FRIENDSHIP, HOSPITALITY, AND CONSISTENT PRESENCE CAN BE A POWERFUL EXPRESSION OF GOD’S LOVE. -AI

ONLINE & GLOBAL CONNECTIONS -AI

  • ONLINE COMMUNITIES AND REMOTE WORK SOMETIMES CONNECT PEOPLE ACROSS COUNTRIES AND CULTURES. -AI
  • EVEN DIGITALLY, LISTENING WELL, RESPECTING DIFFERENCES, AND SHARING HOPE IN CHRIST CAN BE PART OF MINISTRY. -AI

PREPARATION & WISDOM FOR CROSS-CULTURAL SERVICE -AI

WHETHER SERVING LOCALLY OR TRAVELING, PREPARATION AND WISDOM ARE IMPORTANT FOR LOVING OTHERS WELL AND AVOIDING UNINTENDED HARM. -AI

LEARNING ABOUT CULTURE & CONTEXT -AI

  • LEARN ABOUT LOCAL HISTORY, CUSTOMS, AND SENSITIVITIES BEFORE ENTERING A NEW CONTEXT. -AI
  • ASK LOCAL BELIEVERS OR LEADERS HOW OUTSIDERS CAN SERVE IN WAYS THAT ARE HELPFUL, NOT DISRUPTIVE. -AI

LANGUAGE & COMMUNICATION -AI

  • EVEN BASIC GREETINGS, THANK-YOUS, OR KEY PHRASES IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE CAN EXPRESS RESPECT AND CARE. -AI
  • BE PATIENT WITH MISUNDERSTANDINGS, AND USE SIMPLE, CLEAR LANGUAGE WHEN COMMUNICATING ACROSS BARRIERS. -AI

HEALTH, SAFETY & LIMITS -AI

  • CONSIDER HEALTH, SAFETY, AND EMOTIONAL LIMITS REALISTICALLY WHEN EXPLORING TRAVEL OR LONG-TERM SERVICE. -AI
  • SEEK COUNSEL FROM TRUSTED LEADERS, FRIENDS, OR MENTORS AS YOU DISCERN NEXT STEPS. -AI

PARTNERSHIPS, SENDING & SUPPORT -AI

INTERNATIONAL MINISTRY IS OFTEN STRONGEST WHEN IT IS ROOTED IN LOCAL CHURCH COMMUNITY AND HEALTHY PARTNERSHIPS RATHER THAN ISOLATED EFFORTS. -AI

  • CONNECT WITH LOCAL CHURCH LEADERS OR GROUPS WHO HAVE EXPERIENCE IN CROSS-CULTURAL MINISTRY. -AI
  • WHERE POSSIBLE, SERVE ALONGSIDE LOCAL BELIEVERS AND LOCAL CHURCHES RATHER THAN WORKING INDEPENDENTLY. -AI
  • SUPPORT THOSE WHO ARE SENT THROUGH PRAYER, ENCOURAGEMENT, PRACTICAL HELP, AND, WHEN APPROPRIATE, FINANCIAL SUPPORT. -AI
