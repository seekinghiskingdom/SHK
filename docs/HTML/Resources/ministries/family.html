WHAT IS FAMILY & PARENTING MINISTRY? -AI

FAMILY & PARENTING MINISTRY LOOKS AT HOW THE GOSPEL SHAPES LIFE TOGETHER IN THE HOME, INCLUDING MARRIAGE, PARENTING, SIBLING RELATIONSHIPS, AND SHARED HABITS OF WORSHIP. -AI

THIS IS DISTINCT FROM PERSONAL MINISTRY, WHICH FOCUSES ON INDIVIDUAL WALK AND WITNESS; HERE THE FOCUS IS ON “US” RATHER THAN ONLY “ME.” -AI

THIS PAGE IS A FRAMEWORK WHERE SIMPLE GUIDES, READING SUGGESTIONS, AND EXTERNAL RESOURCES CAN BE ADDED OVER TIME TO SUPPORT FAMILIES IN DIFFERENT SEASONS. -AI

FAMILY WORSHIP & RHYTHMS -AI

MANY FAMILIES FIND IT HELPFUL TO BUILD SIMPLE, SUSTAINABLE RHYTHMS THAT CENTER THEIR HOME ON GOD’S WORD, PRAYER, AND SHARED CONVERSATION ABOUT FAITH. -AI

STARTING SMALL -AI

  • BEGIN WITH BRIEF, REGULAR TIMES RATHER THAN LONG SESSIONS THAT ARE HARD TO MAINTAIN. -AI
  • FOR EXAMPLE, A SHORT PASSAGE, A SIMPLE QUESTION, AND A PRAYER A FEW TIMES A WEEK. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SAMPLE OUTLINES FOR SHORT FAMILY WORSHIP TIMES ACROSS DIFFERENT AGES. -AI

USING DIFFERENT AGES WELL -AI

  • MAKE SPACE FOR YOUNGER CHILDREN THROUGH STORIES, QUESTIONS, OR SIMPLE REPETITIONS. -AI
  • GIVE OLDER CHILDREN OR TEENS CHANCES TO READ, PRAY, OR HELP LEAD PARTS OF THE TIME. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: IDEAS FOR ADJUSTING THE SAME BASIC RHYTHM FOR MIXED-AGE FAMILIES. -AI

RHYTHMS, NOT PRESSURE -AI

  • EXPECT INTERRUPTIONS, NOISY DAYS, AND MISSED EVENINGS; CONSISTENT DIRECTION MATTERS MORE THAN PERFECTION. -AI
  • USE MEALTIMES, BEDTIMES, OR CAR RIDES AS NATURAL TOUCHPOINTS FOR PRAYER AND SCRIPTURE. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: SHORT ARTICLES OR TIPS ON NAVIGATING EXPECTATIONS AND GRACE IN FAMILY WORSHIP. -AI

PARENTING & DISCIPLESHIP -AI

PARENTING INVOLVES BOTH CARE AND DISCIPLINE, AND CHRISTIAN PARENTING ALSO AIMS TO INTRODUCE CHILDREN TO CHRIST AND HELP THEM GROW IN KNOWING HIM. -AI

MODEL & EXAMPLE -AI

  • CHILDREN WATCH HOW ADULTS HANDLE FAILURE, STRESS, AND CONFLICT, NOT JUST WHAT THEY SAY. -AI
  • APOLOGIZING, FORGIVING, AND SEEKING GOD OPENLY CAN BECOME POWERFUL EXAMPLES. -AI

CONVERSATIONS ABOUT FAITH -AI

  • INVITE QUESTIONS ABOUT GOD, THE BIBLE, AND LIFE, EVEN WHEN YOU DO NOT HAVE PERFECT ANSWERS. -AI
  • USE BEDTIME, WALKS, OR DRIVE TIMES AS SPACES FOR NATURAL FAITH CONVERSATIONS. -AI

BOUNDARIES & GRACE -AI

  • HEALTHY DISCIPLINE COMBINES CLEAR BOUNDARIES WITH CONSISTENT LOVE AND PATIENCE. -AI
  • REMEMBER THAT GOD IS PATIENT AND FAITHFUL WITH PARENTS TOO; YOU ARE LEARNING AS YOU GO. -AI

  • WHERE POSSIBLE, SEEK WISE COUNSEL FROM LOCAL LEADERS, MENTORS, OR COUNSELORS WHO KNOW YOUR FAMILY. -AI
