HOW TO USE SOCIAL MEDIA RESOURCES WISELY -AI

SOCIAL MEDIA CAN BE A GREAT TOOL, BUT IT CAN ALSO BE OVERWHELMING OR CONFUSING IF WE DO NOT USE IT WITH CARE, DISCERNMENT, AND CLEAR PRIORITIES. -AI

KEEP SCRIPTURE CENTRAL -AI

  • LET THE BIBLE, NOT ALGORITHMS, BE YOUR PRIMARY SOURCE AND STANDARD FOR TRUTH. -AI
  • COMPARE WHAT YOU HEAR ONLINE WITH WHAT YOU READ IN SCRIPTURE AND WITH TRUSTED LOCAL COMMUNITY. -AI

WATCH YOUR INTAKE & EXPECTATIONS -AI

  • SET REASONABLE LIMITS ON TIME SPENT SCROLLING AND FOCUS ON CONTENT THAT TRULY BUILDS YOU UP. -AI
  • REMEMBER THAT SHORT CLIPS CAN BE HELPFUL, BUT RARELY TELL THE WHOLE STORY BY THEMSELVES. -AI

STAY ROOTED IN COMMUNITY -AI

  • USE ONLINE RESOURCES ALONGSIDE LOCAL CHURCH LIFE, FRIENDSHIPS, AND MENTORS WHO KNOW YOU PERSONALLY. -AI
  • BRING QUESTIONS FROM ONLINE CONTENT INTO REAL CONVERSATIONS WHERE YOU CAN PROCESS THEM TOGETHER. -AI
