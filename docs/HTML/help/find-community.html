TYPES OF COMMUNITY YOU MIGHT SEEK -AI

DIFFERENT SEASONS AND SITUATIONS MAY CALL FOR DIFFERENT KINDS OF COMMUNITY. BELOW ARE SEVERAL COMMON CONTEXTS AND WHERE TO LEARN MORE ABOUT EACH. -AI

LOCAL CHURCHES -AI

A LOCAL CHURCH IS OFTEN THE PRIMARY PLACE WHERE PEOPLE GATHER REGULARLY FOR WORSHIP, TEACHING, PRAYER, AND COMMUNITY LIFE. -AI

  • LOOK FOR A CHURCH WHERE SCRIPTURE IS HONORED, THE GOSPEL IS CLEAR, AND PEOPLE ARE WELCOMED WITH GRACE AND TRUTH. -AI
  • IF POSSIBLE, VISIT MORE THAN ONCE TO GET A CLEARER SENSE OF ITS LIFE AND HEALTH. -AI

SMALL GROUPS & HOME GATHERINGS -AI

SMALL GROUPS, HOME GROUPS, AND BIBLE STUDIES PROVIDE SPACES FOR DEEPER RELATIONSHIPS, DISCUSSION, AND MUTUAL ENCOURAGEMENT. -AI

  • THEY MAY BE RUN THROUGH A LOCAL CHURCH OR INFORMALLY AMONG FRIENDS OR NEIGHBORS. -AI
  • GROUPS CAN FOCUS ON BIBLE STUDY, PRAYER, ACCOUNTABILITY, OR SPECIFIC LIFE STAGES. -AI

  • THESE GROUPS OFTEN MEET ON OR NEAR CAMPUSES FOR WORSHIP, STUDY, AND FELLOWSHIP. -AI

PRACTICAL NEXT STEPS TO TAKE -AI

IF YOU ARE UNSURE WHERE TO BEGIN, SMALL, CLEAR ACTIONS CAN MAKE THE PROCESS FEEL LESS OVERWHELMING. -AI

  1. STEP 1 – CLARIFY WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING FOR. -AI
    WRITE DOWN WHETHER YOU ARE SEEKING A CHURCH, A SMALL GROUP, A CAMPUS MINISTRY, ONLINE SUPPORT, OR SOMETHING ELSE. -AI
  2. STEP 2 – CHECK LOCAL OPTIONS. -AI
    SEARCH FOR CHURCHES OR GROUPS NEAR YOU, ASK TRUSTED FRIENDS OR LEADERS FOR SUGGESTIONS, OR USE FIND-A-CHURCH OR SIMILAR DIRECTORIES WHERE AVAILABLE. -AI
  3. STEP 3 – VISIT OR TRY A GATHERING. -AI
    ATTEND A SERVICE OR GROUP MEETING WHEN YOU CAN, TAKING NOTE OF HOW SCRIPTURE, COMMUNITY, AND CARE ARE HANDLED. -AI
  4. STEP 4 – GIVE IT SOME TIME. -AI
    IF IT SEEMS HEALTHY BUT UNFAMILIAR, CONSIDER VISITING MORE THAN ONCE BEFORE DECIDING. IF SOMETHING SEEMS CONCERNING, IT IS OKAY TO STEP BACK AND SEEK COUNSEL. -AI
  5. STEP 5 – REACH OUT IF YOU NEED GUIDANCE. -AI
    IF YOU ARE STUCK OR NEED SOMEONE TO PRAY WITH YOU ABOUT COMMUNITY, YOU CAN USE THE CONTACT OR PRAYER & TESTIMONY PAGES TO ASK FOR SUPPORT. -AI

THESE STEPS ARE NOT RULES BUT SIMPLE GUIDELINES; IT IS NORMAL FOR THE PROCESS TO TAKE TIME AND INVOLVE SOME TRIAL AND ERROR. -AI

WISDOM, SAFETY & DISCERNMENT -AI

SEEKING COMMUNITY IS IMPORTANT, BUT IT IS ALSO IMPORTANT TO BE WISE ABOUT ENVIRONMENTS, LEADERSHIP, AND RELATIONSHIPS. -AI

  • PAY ATTENTION TO HOW PEOPLE ARE TREATED, ESPECIALLY THOSE WHO ARE VULNERABLE OR NEW. -AI
  • NOTICE WHETHER SCRIPTURE IS HANDLED CAREFULLY AND JESUS IS CENTRAL, NOT JUST A SIDE LABEL. -AI
  • BE CAUTIOUS ABOUT GROUPS OR INDIVIDUALS WHO DEMAND SECRECY, ISOLATE YOU FROM OTHER BELIEVERS, OR PRESSURE YOU INTO RASH DECISIONS. -AI
  • IF SOMETHING FEELS CONFUSING OR HEAVY, IT CAN HELP TO TALK WITH A TRUSTED CHRISTIAN FRIEND, MENTOR, OR LEADER OUTSIDE THAT CONTEXT. -AI

THIS PAGE CANNOT COVER EVERY SITUATION, BUT IT IS OKAY TO SLOW DOWN, ASK QUESTIONS, AND SEEK WISDOM BEFORE COMMITTING TO A NEW COMMUNITY. -AI
