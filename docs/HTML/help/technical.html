WHAT COUNTS AS A TECHNICAL ISSUE? -AI

A TECHNICAL ISSUE IS ANYTHING THAT SEEMS TO BE WRONG WITH HOW THE SITE WORKS, RATHER THAN A QUESTION ABOUT CONTENT OR THEOLOGY. -AI

FOR EXAMPLE, IF A PAGE DOES NOT LOAD, A TOOL DOES NOT RESPOND WHEN YOU CLICK, OR A FORM WILL NOT SUBMIT, THAT IS USUALLY A TECHNICAL ISSUE. -AI

  • PAGES THAT LOAD BLANK, HALF-LOADED, OR WITH OBVIOUS LAYOUT BREAKS. -AI
  • BUTTONS, LINKS, OR TABS THAT DO NOTHING WHEN CLICKED. -AI
  • FORMS THAT WILL NOT SUBMIT OR SHOW UNEXPECTED ERROR MESSAGES. -AI
  • TOOLS OR GAMES THAT FREEZE, CRASH, OR DO NOT BEHAVE AS DESCRIBED. -AI

IF YOU ARE MAINLY COMMENTING ON DESIGN CHOICES, CONTENT CLARITY, OR FEATURE IDEAS, THE GENERAL FEEDBACK FORM MAY BE A BETTER FIT. -AI

COMMON TECHNICAL ISSUES YOU MIGHT SEE -AI

MANY PROBLEMS FALL INTO A FEW FAMILIES OF ISSUES. RECOGNIZING THEM CAN HELP YOU TROUBLESHOOT OR DESCRIBE THEM MORE CLEARLY WHEN REPORTING. -AI

PAGES NOT LOADING OR VERY SLOW -AI

  • BLANK PAGE OR “PAGE NOT FOUND” MESSAGE. -AI
  • PAGES TAKING A LONG TIME TO APPEAR OR ONLY PARTIALLY LOADING. -AI
  • IMAGES OR STYLES (COLORS, FONTS) NOT APPEARING CORRECTLY. -AI

BUTTONS, LINKS, OR TABS NOT WORKING -AI

  • CLICKING A BUTTON DOES NOTHING AND THE PAGE DOES NOT CHANGE. -AI
  • TABS DO NOT SWITCH CONTENT OR SCROLL TO THE RIGHT SECTION. -AI
  • DOWNLOAD OR EXPORT BUTTONS DO NOT START A DOWNLOAD. -AI

FORMS & SUBMISSIONS FAILING -AI

  • GOOGLE FORMS OR OTHER FORMS NOT LOADING OR NOT ACCEPTING INPUT. -AI
  • ERROR MESSAGES WHEN TRYING TO SUBMIT FEEDBACK OR CONTACT INFO. -AI
  • TEXT FIELDS OR CHECKBOXES NOT RESPONDING AS EXPECTED. -AI

TOOLS & GAMES MISBEHAVING -AI

  • BIBLE TOOLS OR SEARCH TOOLS NOT SHOWING RESULTS. -AI
  • GAMES OR INTERACTIVE FEATURES FREEZING MID-USE. -AI
  • UNEXPECTED POPUPS, ERRORS, OR UNUSUAL BEHAVIOR AFTER A FEW CLICKS. -AI

BASIC TROUBLESHOOTING TO TRY FIRST -AI

BEFORE REPORTING A TECHNICAL ISSUE, IT CAN HELP TO TRY A FEW SIMPLE STEPS. SOMETIMES THESE FIX THE PROBLEM OR MAKE IT EASIER TO DESCRIBE. -AI

  1. STEP 1 – REFRESH THE PAGE. -AI
    CLICK REFRESH/RELOAD IN YOUR BROWSER AND SEE IF THE PROBLEM PERSISTS. -AI
  2. STEP 2 – TRY A DIFFERENT PAGE OR SECTION. -AI
    CHECK WHETHER OTHER PAGES ON THE SITE WORK NORMALLY, OR IF THE ISSUE IS SITE-WIDE. -AI
  3. STEP 3 – TRY A DIFFERENT DEVICE OR BROWSER (IF POSSIBLE). -AI
    SOMETIMES A PROBLEM AFFECTS ONLY A SPECIFIC BROWSER OR DEVICE TYPE. -AI
  4. STEP 4 – CLEAR CACHED VERSION OR USE PRIVATE/INCOGNITO MODE. -AI
    OPEN THE PAGE IN A PRIVATE/INCOGNITO WINDOW TO SEE IF AN OLD CACHED VERSION IS CAUSING ISSUES. -AI
  5. STEP 5 – CHECK INTERNET CONNECTION. -AI
    MAKE SURE YOUR CONNECTION IS STABLE, ESPECIALLY IF PAGES ARE PARTIALLY LOADING OR TIMING OUT. -AI

IF THE PROBLEM CONTINUES AFTER THESE STEPS, INCLUDING THIS INFORMATION IN YOUR REPORT CAN BE VERY HELPFUL. -AI

HOW TO REPORT A TECHNICAL PROBLEM -AI

WHEN YOU REPORT A TECHNICAL ISSUE, A FEW KEY DETAILS MAKE IT MUCH EASIER TO UNDERSTAND AND REPRODUCE THE PROBLEM. -AI

WHAT TO INCLUDE IN YOUR REPORT -AI

  • WHICH PAGE OR TOOL YOU WERE USING (URL OR TITLE). -AI
  • WHAT YOU WERE TRYING TO DO, STEP BY STEP. -AI
  • WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED (INCLUDING ANY ERROR MESSAGES). -AI
  • WHICH DEVICE AND BROWSER YOU WERE USING (IF YOU KNOW). -AI
  • WHETHER YOU TRIED BASIC TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS YET. -AI

WHERE TO SEND TECHNICAL REPORTS -AI

  • FOR MOST TECHNICAL ISSUES, USE THE MAIN FEEDBACK FORM AND SELECT THE BUGS/ERRORS OR TECHNICAL SECTION:
    OPEN FEEDBACK & SUGGESTIONS PAGE -AI
  • IF YOUR ISSUE IS PRIMARILY ABOUT HOW A PAGE LOOKS OR BEHAVES, YOU CAN ALSO MENTION IT ON THE DESIGN OR NAVIGATION PAGES OF THE FORM. -AI
  • IF YOU PREFER A SIMPLE MESSAGE, YOU CAN DESCRIBE THE ISSUE ON THE CONTACT PAGE: CONTACT US -AI

KNOWN OR RECURRING ISSUES (PLACEHOLDER) -AI
