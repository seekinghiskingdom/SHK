FEEDBACK FORM PAGES & TOPICS -AI

THE MAIN FEEDBACK FORM IS DIVIDED INTO PAGES SO YOU CAN FOCUS ON SPECIFIC AREAS. THE PAGES ARE LISTED BELOW IN THE SAME ORDER AS IN THE FORM. -AI

PAGE 1: INFO & OVERALL EXPERIENCE -AI

SHARE YOUR GENERAL IMPRESSION OF THE SITE, INCLUDING OVERALL EXPERIENCE, CLARITY, AND HOW EASY IT IS TO FIND WHAT YOU NEED. -AI

PAGE 2: GENERAL FEEDBACK -AI

PROVIDE SPECIFIC COMMENTS ON CONTENT QUALITY, HELPFULNESS, AND ANY BROAD OBSERVATIONS THAT DO NOT FIT A MORE NARROW CATEGORY. -AI

PAGE 3: BUGS & ERRORS -AI

REPORT TECHNICAL ISSUES, BROKEN LINKS, MISSING PAGES, TYPOGRAPHICAL ERRORS, OR ANYTHING THAT DOES NOT APPEAR TO BE WORKING AS INTENDED. -AI

PAGE 4: FEATURE REQUESTS -AI

SUGGEST NEW FEATURES, TOOLS, OR OPTIONS YOU WOULD FIND HELPFUL, OR IMPROVEMENTS TO EXISTING FEATURES YOU ALREADY USE. -AI

PAGE 5: CONTENT SUGGESTIONS -AI

RECOMMEND NEW TOPICS, ARTICLES, PASSAGES, OR TYPES OF RESOURCES THAT WOULD MAKE THE SITE MORE USEFUL FOR YOU OR OTHERS. -AI

PAGE 6: DESIGN & USABILITY -AI

OFFER FEEDBACK ON COLORS, LAYOUT, READABILITY, BUTTONS, AND HOW NATURAL OR CONFUSING IT FEELS TO MOVE AROUND THE SITE VISUALLY. -AI

PAGE 7: NAVIGATION & STRUCTURE -AI

COMMENT ON HOW THE SITE IS ORGANIZED, HOW EASY IT IS TO FIND PAGES, AND WHETHER THE MENUS, HUBS, AND LINKS MAKE SENSE. -AI

PAGE 8: ACCESSIBILITY -AI

SHARE HOW ACCESSIBLE THE SITE IS FOR YOU, INCLUDING SCREEN READERS, KEYBOARD NAVIGATION, CONTRAST, FONT SIZE, AND OTHER ACCESS NEEDS. -AI

PAGE 9: TOOLS -AI

PROVIDE FEEDBACK ON ANY TOOLS OR INTERACTIVE FEATURES (SUCH AS BIBLE VIEWERS, DAILY TOOLS, SEARCH TOOLS, OR LANGUAGE RESOURCES). -AI

PAGE 10: GAMES -AI

SUGGEST IMPROVEMENTS OR NEW IDEAS FOR GAMES OR GAME-LIKE EXPERIENCES RELATED TO SCRIPTURE, LEARNING, OR PRACTICAL APPLICATION. -AI

PAGE 11: PERSONAL FEEDBACK -AI

SHARE MORE PERSONAL REFLECTIONS, TESTIMONIES ABOUT YOUR EXPERIENCE USING THE SITE, OR WAYS GOD HAS USED THESE RESOURCES IN YOUR LIFE. -AI

YOU DO NOT NEED TO FILL OUT EVERY PAGE. YOU CAN COMPLETE ONLY THE PARTS THAT APPLY TO YOU AND SUBMIT THE FORM WHENEVER YOU ARE READY. -AI

HOW YOUR FEEDBACK IS USED -AI

FEEDBACK IS REVIEWED PERIODICALLY TO IDENTIFY COMMON THEMES, TRACK BUGS, AND PRIORITIZE CHANGES OR NEW FEATURES. -AI

  • BUG REPORTS AND TECHNICAL ISSUES MAY BE TRACKED IN A SIMPLE INTERNAL LIST UNTIL THEY ARE RESOLVED. -AI
  • FEATURE REQUESTS ARE NOT PROMISES, BUT THEY HELP SHAPE A ROADMAP FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT. -AI
  • CONTENT AND DESIGN SUGGESTIONS INFORM HOW PAGES ARE UPDATED OR NEW SECTIONS ARE CREATED. -AI
  • PERSONAL FEEDBACK AND TESTIMONIES MAY BE KEPT PRIVATE UNLESS EXPLICIT PERMISSION IS GIVEN TO SHARE THEM. -AI
