WHY SHARE PRAYER REQUESTS & TESTIMONIES? -AI

MANY PEOPLE LONG TO KNOW THEY ARE NOT ALONE IN THEIR STRUGGLES AND JOYS, AND SHARED PRAYER AND TESTIMONY CAN BE A POWERFUL WAY TO EXPERIENCE THAT. -AI

ASKING OTHERS TO PRAY IS NOT A SIGN OF WEAKNESS, BUT AN EXPRESSION OF TRUST, AND SHARING TESTIMONIES OR BLESSINGS CAN ENCOURAGE OTHERS TO KEEP TRUSTING GOD IN THEIR OWN STORIES. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES SIMPLE PATHS FOR SUBMITTING THESE THINGS, WITH CAREFUL ATTENTION TO PRIVACY, BOUNDARIES, AND EXPECTATIONS. -AI

WHAT YOU CAN SHARE HERE -AI

SUBMISSIONS GENERALLY FALL INTO A FEW BROAD CATEGORIES. YOU CAN SHARE AS MUCH OR AS LITTLE DETAIL AS YOU ARE COMFORTABLE WITH. -AI

PRAYER REQUESTS -AI

  • PERSONAL STRUGGLES, QUESTIONS, OR NEEDS WHERE YOU WOULD LIKE OTHERS TO PRAY. -AI
  • REQUESTS FOR FAMILY, FRIENDS, LOCAL COMMUNITIES, OR WIDER SITUATIONS. -AI
  • ONGOING SITUATIONS WHERE CONTINUED PRAYER IS HELPFUL. -AI

TESTIMONIES -AI

  • STORIES OF HOW GOD HAS WORKED IN YOUR LIFE, WHETHER DRAMATIC OR QUIET. -AI
  • MOMENTS OF ANSWERED PRAYER, GROWTH, OR NEW UNDERSTANDING. -AI
  • EXPERIENCES OF GOD’S FAITHFULNESS IN DAILY LIFE OR LONG SEASONS. -AI

BLESSINGS & PRAISE REPORTS -AI

  • WAYS YOU HAVE SEEN GOD’S GOODNESS IN BIG OR SMALL WAYS. -AI
  • ENCOURAGEMENTS YOU WANT TO SHARE TO BUILD OTHERS UP. -AI
  • SHORT NOTES OF THANKFULNESS OR PRAISE. -AI

IT IS OKAY IF YOU ARE UNSURE WHICH CATEGORY YOUR MESSAGE FITS; YOU CAN SIMPLY SHARE WHAT IS ON YOUR HEART AND LET THE FORM HANDLE THE REST. -AI

HOW TO SUBMIT PRAYER REQUESTS, TESTIMONIES & BLESSINGS -AI

THERE ARE A FEW SIMPLE PATHS FOR SHARING, DEPENDING ON WHAT IS CURRENTLY AVAILABLE AND WHAT YOU PREFER. -AI

PRAYER & TESTIMONY FORM -AI

THE PRIMARY WAY TO SUBMIT PRAYER REQUESTS, TESTIMONIES, OR BLESSINGS IS THROUGH A SIMPLE FORM. -AI

  • CHOOSE WHETHER YOU ARE SHARING A PRAYER REQUEST, TESTIMONY, BLESSING, OR MIX OF THESE. -AI
  • WRITE YOUR MESSAGE IN YOUR OWN WORDS; IT DOES NOT NEED TO BE PERFECT. -AI
  • OPTIONALLY INDICATE IF YOU ARE COMFORTABLE WITH PARTS OF IT BEING SHARED MORE BROADLY IN THE FUTURE. -AI
  • INCLUDE CONTACT DETAILS ONLY IF YOU WOULD LIKE A POSSIBLE FOLLOW-UP WHEN CAPACITY ALLOWS. -AI

HOW YOUR SUBMISSION MAY BE SHARED OR USED -AI

SOME SUBMISSIONS MAY BE KEPT PRIVATE, WHILE OTHERS MAY EVENTUALLY BE SHARED IN MORE PUBLIC WAYS (SUCH AS ANONYMOUS BOARDS OR ENCOURAGEMENT PAGES), DEPENDING ON YOUR PREFERENCES AND FUTURE FEATURES. -AI

  • YOU CAN INDICATE WHETHER YOU PREFER YOUR MESSAGE TO BE:
    • KEPT PRIVATE (FOR INTERNAL PRAYER ONLY). -AI
    • SHARED ANONYMOUSLY (NO NAME OR IDENTIFYING DETAILS). -AI
    • SHARED WITH FIRST NAME OR A SIMPLE IDENTIFIER, IF YOU ARE COMFORTABLE. -AI
  • PERSONAL OR IDENTIFYING DETAILS MAY BE MINIMIZED OR EDITED BEFORE ANY PUBLIC SHARING, EVEN IF YOU GRANT PERMISSION. -AI
  • SUBMISSIONS MAY BE SUMMARIZED OR GROUPED (FOR EXAMPLE, “SEVERAL PEOPLE ASKED FOR PRAYER ABOUT HEALTH, FAMILY, OR WORK”). -AI
  • NO SUBMISSION IS GUARANTEED TO BE FEATURED OR POSTED; CAPACITY AND WISDOM WILL GUIDE WHAT IS SHARED. -AI

IMPORTANT LIMITS & BOUNDARIES -AI

THIS SPACE IS MEANT TO BE CARING AND PRAYERFUL, BUT THERE ARE IMPORTANT LIMITS TO WHAT CAN BE OFFERED THROUGH AN ONLINE FORM OR WEBSITE. -AI

  • SUBMISSIONS MAY NOT RECEIVE AN INDIVIDUAL REPLY, EVEN IF THEY ARE READ AND PRAYED OVER. -AI
  • THIS PAGE AND ITS FORMS DO NOT PROVIDE PROFESSIONAL COUNSELING, LEGAL, OR MEDICAL ADVICE. -AI
  • IF A MESSAGE SUGGESTS SOMEONE IS IN IMMEDIATE DANGER OR AT RISK OF HARM, ENCOURAGING CONTACT WITH LOCAL AUTHORITIES OR CRISIS SERVICES MAY BE NECESSARY. -AI
  • FOR URGENT OR EMERGENCY SITUATIONS, PLEASE SEE THE CRISIS & IMMEDIATE HELP PAGE AND CONTACT LOCAL RESOURCES DIRECTLY. -AI
