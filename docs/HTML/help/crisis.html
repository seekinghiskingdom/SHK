READ THIS FIRST -AI

IF YOU ARE IN CRISIS, YOU DO NOT HAVE TO WALK THROUGH IT ALONE, BUT IT IS IMPORTANT TO REACH OUT TO SOMEONE WHO CAN RESPOND DIRECTLY AND SAFELY IN REAL TIME. -AI

THIS PAGE OFFERS GENERAL GUIDANCE AND SUGGESTIONS, BUT IT IS NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR LOCAL EMERGENCY SERVICES, CRISIS LINES, OR PROFESSIONAL CARE. -AI

IF YOU ARE UNSURE WHETHER YOUR SITUATION “COUNTS” AS A CRISIS, IT IS USUALLY SAFER TO REACH OUT THAN TO STAY SILENT. -AI

IF YOU ARE IN IMMEDIATE DANGER -AI

IF YOU OR SOMEONE NEAR YOU IS IN IMMEDIATE DANGER, OR YOU BELIEVE THERE IS A RISK OF SERIOUS HARM IN THE NEXT MOMENTS OR HOURS, PLEASE TAKE THE STEPS BELOW RIGHT AWAY. -AI

  • CONTACT YOUR LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER (SUCH AS 911 IN THE UNITED STATES) OR EQUIVALENT SERVICES IN YOUR COUNTRY IMMEDIATELY. -AI
  • IF POSSIBLE, MOVE TO A SAFER LOCATION OR STAY WITH SOMEONE YOU TRUST WHILE YOU MAKE THE CALL. -AI
  • IF YOU CANNOT SAFELY CALL, CONSIDER TEXT OR ONLINE EMERGENCY OPTIONS WHERE AVAILABLE IN YOUR REGION. -AI

THIS WEBSITE CANNOT SEE OR RESPOND TO EMERGENCIES IN REAL TIME, AND MESSAGES SENT THROUGH CONTACT OR FEEDBACK FORMS MAY NOT BE READ QUICKLY. -AI

IF YOU ARE IN THE UNITED STATES, YOU CAN ALSO REACH A 24/7 SUICIDE AND CRISIS HOTLINE (SUCH AS 988) FOR IMMEDIATE SUPPORT AND GUIDANCE. -AI

IF YOU ARE OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES, CHECK FOR LOCAL OR NATIONAL CRISIS LINES, MENTAL HEALTH HELPLINES, OR EMERGENCY NUMBERS PROVIDED BY YOUR COUNTRY OR REGION. -AI

IF YOU ARE IN EMOTIONAL CRISIS BUT NOT IN IMMEDIATE DANGER -AI

SOMETIMES YOU MAY FEEL OVERWHELMED, HOPELESS, OR DEEPLY DISTRESSED, EVEN IF THERE IS NO IMMEDIATE THREAT TO YOUR PHYSICAL SAFETY. -AI

IN THOSE MOMENTS, IT CAN STILL BE IMPORTANT TO REACH OUT AND NOT CARRY EVERYTHING ALONE. -AI

  1. STEP 1 – REACH OUT TO SOMEONE YOU TRUST. -AI
    THIS COULD BE A FRIEND, FAMILY MEMBER, PASTOR, COUNSELOR, OR OTHER TRUSTED PERSON WHO CAN LISTEN AND BE PRESENT WITH YOU. -AI
  2. STEP 2 – CONSIDER A CRISIS OR SUPPORT LINE. -AI
    MANY COUNTRIES HAVE HELPLINES WHERE TRAINED LISTENERS CAN TALK OR CHAT WITH YOU, EVEN IF YOU ARE NOT IN IMMEDIATE DANGER. -AI
  3. STEP 3 – LIMIT ISOLATION IF POSSIBLE. -AI
    IF YOU CAN, AVOID STAYING COMPLETELY ALONE DURING A STRONG CRISIS; EVEN SITTING NEAR OTHERS IN A PUBLIC PLACE MAY HELP MOVE YOU TOWARD SAFER SUPPORT. -AI
  4. STEP 4 – TAKE SMALL, GROUNDING ACTIONS. -AI
    SIMPLE ACTIONS LIKE SLOW BREATHING, DRINKING WATER, OR NAMING THINGS AROUND YOU CAN HELP YOUR BODY BEGIN TO DE-ESCALATE WHILE YOU SEEK FURTHER HELP. -AI

WHO YOU MIGHT CONTACT FOR HELP -AI

DIFFERENT SITUATIONS MAY CALL FOR DIFFERENT KINDS OF SUPPORT. YOU DO NOT HAVE TO CHOOSE ONLY ONE; SOMETIMES A COMBINATION IS MOST HELPFUL. -AI

EMERGENCY SERVICES -AI

WHEN THERE IS A RISK OF SERIOUS OR IMMEDIATE HARM, LOCAL EMERGENCY SERVICES ARE USUALLY THE MOST DIRECT WAY TO RECEIVE URGENT HELP. -AI

CRISIS LINES & HELPLINES -AI

MANY REGIONS HAVE CONFIDENTIAL HOTLINES FOR SUICIDAL THOUGHTS, SELF-HARM, ABUSE, OR OTHER KINDS OF CRISIS, OFTEN AVAILABLE BY PHONE, TEXT, OR CHAT. -AI

TRUSTED PEOPLE IN YOUR LIFE -AI

FRIENDS, FAMILY, MENTORS, OR PASTORS WHO CARE ABOUT YOU CAN BE IMPORTANT FIRST CONTACTS, EVEN IF THEY ARE NOT PROFESSIONAL COUNSELORS. -AI

PROFESSIONAL CARE -AI

THERAPISTS, COUNSELORS, DOCTORS, AND OTHER HEALTH PROFESSIONALS CAN OFFER SPECIALIZED SUPPORT, ESPECIALLY FOR ONGOING STRUGGLES WITH MENTAL OR EMOTIONAL HEALTH. -AI

IF YOU ARE UNSURE WHERE TO START, A CRISIS LINE OR A TRUSTED LOCAL LEADER (SUCH AS A PASTOR OR COMMUNITY LEADER) CAN SOMETIMES HELP YOU FIND THE NEXT RIGHT CONTACT. -AI

FAITH, PRAYER & COMMUNITY SUPPORT -AI

SPIRITUAL SUPPORT CAN BE VERY IMPORTANT IN TIMES OF CRISIS, BUT IT IS MEANT TO WORK ALONGSIDE, NOT INSTEAD OF, APPROPRIATE SAFETY AND CARE. -AI

  • IF YOU HAVE A LOCAL CHURCH OR CHRISTIAN COMMUNITY, CONSIDER REACHING OUT TO A LEADER OR TRUSTED MEMBER FOR PRAYER AND PRACTICAL SUPPORT. -AI
  • IF YOU DO NOT HAVE LOCAL COMMUNITY, YOU MAY STILL BE ABLE TO FIND ONLINE GROUPS OR CHURCHES NEAR YOU THAT CAN HELP OVER TIME. -AI
  • WRITING OUT YOUR PRAYER OR CRY TO GOD, EVEN IN SIMPLE WORDS, CAN SOMETIMES HELP GIVE LANGUAGE TO WHAT YOU ARE FEELING. -AI

BASIC SAFETY STEPS YOU CAN TAKE RIGHT NOW -AI

THE FOLLOWING IDEAS ARE GENERAL AND MAY NOT FIT EVERY SITUATION, BUT THEY CAN SOMETIMES HELP YOU STAY SAFER WHILE YOU SEEK FURTHER HELP. -AI

  1. STEP 1 – REDUCE IMMEDIATE RISKS AROUND YOU. -AI
    IF IT IS SAFE TO DO SO, CREATE SOME DISTANCE FROM ANY OBJECTS OR SITUATIONS THAT MIGHT MAKE IT EASIER TO HARM YOURSELF OR OTHERS. -AI
  2. STEP 2 – LET SOMEONE KNOW HOW YOU ARE DOING. -AI
    EVEN A SHORT MESSAGE LIKE “I AM NOT OKAY RIGHT NOW, CAN WE TALK?” CAN OPEN A DOOR FOR SUPPORT. -AI
  3. STEP 3 – FOCUS ON THE NEXT SMALL STEP, NOT THE WHOLE FUTURE. -AI
    TRY TO IDENTIFY JUST ONE NEXT SAFE ACTION, SUCH AS MAKING A PHONE CALL, WALKING TO A PUBLIC PLACE, OR DRINKING SOME WATER. -AI
  4. STEP 4 – BREATHE SLOWLY AND NAME WHAT YOU SEE. -AI
    TAKE SLOW BREATHS AND QUIETLY NAME A FEW THINGS YOU CAN SEE, HEAR, OR TOUCH AROUND YOU TO HELP YOUR BODY BEGIN TO GROUND. -AI
  5. STEP 5 – PLAN TO FOLLOW UP WITH LONGER-TERM SUPPORT. -AI
    WHEN YOU ARE A LITTLE MORE STABLE, CONSIDER REACHING OUT TO A COUNSELOR, DOCTOR, OR PASTOR TO TALK ABOUT ONGOING SUPPORT AND CARE. -AI
