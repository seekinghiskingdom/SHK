QUESTIONS ABOUT USING THE SITE -AI

HOW DO I KNOW WHERE TO START ON THIS SITE? -AI

IF YOU FEEL OVERWHELMED OR ARE NEW TO CHRISTIAN RESOURCES IN GENERAL, THE BEST PLACE TO BEGIN IS THE “WHERE DO I START?” PAGE. -AI

IT GIVES A SIMPLE OVERVIEW OF THE MOST IMPORTANT PAGES AND SUGGESTED NEXT STEPS BASED ON WHY YOU ARE HERE. -AI

YOU CAN FIND IT HERE: WHERE DO I START? – OVERVIEW & FIRST STEPS -AI

HOW DO I FIND A SPECIFIC PAGE OR SECTION? -AI

MOST MAIN SECTIONS ARE ACCESSIBLE THROUGH THE TOP NAVIGATION BAR AND THROUGH HUB PAGES (SUCH AS HOME, RESOURCES, LITERATURE, COMMUNITY, AND SERVING). -AI

YOU CAN ALSO USE THE LEFT-SIDE NAVIGATION CALLOUT (WHEN VISIBLE) TO JUMP BACK UP THE PATH OF WHERE YOU ARE WITHIN THE SITE STRUCTURE. -AI

IF YOU STILL CANNOT FIND A PAGE, YOU CAN USE THE FEEDBACK OR CONTACT PAGES TO ASK FOR HELP OR SUGGEST A FUTURE SEARCH FEATURE. -AI

WHY DO SOME PAGES LOOK LIKE “HUBS” WITH TILES AND OTHERS LOOK SIMPLE? -AI

HUB PAGES ARE DESIGNED AS OVERVIEWS THAT LINK TO SEVERAL RELATED PAGES OR TOOLS, USING VISUAL TILES OR CARDS TO ORGANIZE OPTIONS. -AI

OTHER PAGES ARE FOCUSED ON A SINGLE TOPIC OR RESOURCE AND THEREFORE USE A SIMPLER LAYOUT WITH MOSTLY TEXT AND A FEW KEY LINKS. -AI

WHAT DOES IT MEAN WHEN TEXT ENDS WITH “-AI”? -AI

TEXT THAT ENDS WITH “-AI” INDICATES CONTENT THAT WAS INITIALIZED OR DRAFTED WITH THE HELP OF AN ASSISTANT AND IS INTENDED TO BE REVIEWED OR REFINED OVER TIME. -AI

AS TEXT IS REVIEWED AND FINALIZED, THE “-AI” SUFFIX WILL BE REMOVED, BUT THE UNDERLYING IDEA MAY STILL HAVE BEEN ORIGINALLY ASSISTED. -AI

WHY DO SOME LINKS SAY “COMING SOON”? -AI

SOME FEATURES, TOOLS, OR PAGES ARE PLANNED BUT NOT FULLY AVAILABLE YET. “COMING SOON” IS USED TO MARK AREAS WHERE THE STRUCTURE IS IN PLACE BUT THE ACTUAL FUNCTIONALITY OR CONTENT IS STILL BEING BUILT. -AI

YOU ARE WELCOME TO SUGGEST PRIORITIES OR IDEAS FOR THESE AREAS USING THE FEEDBACK FORM. -AI

QUESTIONS ABOUT ACCOUNTS & PRIVACY -AI

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT TO USE THIS SITE? -AI

CURRENTLY, THE CORE CONTENT AND MOST TOOLS ARE DESIGNED TO BE AVAILABLE WITHOUT CREATING A PERSONAL ACCOUNT. -AI

IF ACCOUNT-BASED FEATURES ARE ADDED IN THE FUTURE (SUCH AS SAVED PROGRESS OR PERSONAL DASHBOARDS), THAT INFORMATION WILL BE CLEARLY EXPLAINED AND LINKED FROM THIS SECTION. -AI

WHAT HAPPENS TO INFORMATION I SUBMIT THROUGH FORMS? -AI

FEEDBACK, CONTACT, AND OTHER FORM SUBMISSIONS ARE USED PRIMARILY TO IMPROVE THE SITE, RESPOND WHEN POSSIBLE, AND UNDERSTAND HOW PEOPLE ARE USING THESE RESOURCES. -AI

PERSONALLY IDENTIFYING INFORMATION IS KEPT TO WHAT YOU PROVIDE, AND CERTAIN DETAILS (LIKE NAMES OR EMAILS) MAY BE MINIMIZED IN INTERNAL RECORDS IF NOT NEEDED FOR FOLLOW-UP. -AI
