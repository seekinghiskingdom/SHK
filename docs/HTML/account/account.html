My Salvation Testimony Private

Do you know what your testimony is?

What is this testimony for?

This is your short Salvation / Life testimony—how Jesus saved you and what that means in your life. Think of it as a clear, simple story you could share with someone in a few minutes: what life was like before, how you came to trust Christ, and how He is at work in you now.

How long should it be?

Aim for a short, focused story (about 1–3 paragraphs, max 2000 characters). You don’t need every detail—just enough context for someone to understand what God has done and what changed. You can always update this later as you grow.

Need help figuring out what your testimony is?

Here are some questions to consider!
- "What was your life like before accepting Christ?"
- “How did you come to know Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior?”
- "What led you to make the decision to follow Him?"
- "Why do you believe He gives salvation?"

- "What has He done in your life since then?"
- "How has your faith grown and what challenges have you faced?"
- "What impact has your relationship with Jesus had on your life and those around you?"
- "How is He working in your life today?"

- "What would you like others to know about your walk with Christ?"
- "What message would you like to share with others to strengthen their faith?"

Guidelines for writing your testimony

Tips as you write:
– Keep Jesus at the center more than yourself.
– Be honest, but avoid unnecessary graphic detail about sin, abuse, or violence.
– Be careful with other people’s privacy (no full names or identifying details without their consent).
– It’s okay if your story doesn’t feel “dramatic.” Salvation is always a miracle, whether it looks quiet or big on the outside.
– If you’re still figuring out what you believe, you can share honestly where you are now and what you’re seeking.

Content boundaries (what is not allowed):
To protect everyone who reads and shares here, testimonies must not include:
– Profanity or explicit sexual content.
– Hateful or demeaning language toward any person or group.
– Slander, attacks, or shaming specific people, churches, or ministries by name.
– Political campaigning, rants, or partisan arguments.
– Detailed how-to descriptions of self-harm, abuse, or illegal activity.
These kinds of content may be blocked or removed even if you try to keep the testimony private.

About moderation and safety checks:
All salvation testimonies run through automatic profanity/sensitivity checks. Sometimes it may be strict or make mistakes, but its goal is to keep this space safe and Christ-honoring.
– If your testimony is flagged, it will not be saved, and you’ll be asked to edit it.
– In some cases, moderators may also review or temporarily restrict content that seems unsafe, harmful, or clearly outside these guidelines.
– Repeated or intentional violations can affect your ability to post publicly or use parts of the site.
