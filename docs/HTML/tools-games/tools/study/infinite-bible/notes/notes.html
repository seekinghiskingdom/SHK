Infinite Bible Notes – Notes Mode

Create free-form boards of verses and notes on a large canvas for topical studies, Proverbs pairs, sermons, and more.

Bible Mode Notes Mode

Early version: boards and layouts are stored locally in your browser on this device. No accounts or sync yet.

Your boards

Boards are per-device. A soft limit will help keep things stable if you create many boards.

Settings & backup

Exporting creates a file you can store or move to another device. Importing will add that board here.

Quick help
  • Create a board for each topic, project, or study you want to keep separate.
  • Use “Insert passage” to add verses to the canvas, then drag them into clusters.
  • Add notes around verses to capture observations, connections, and questions.
  • Boards save automatically in this browser; reopen Notes Mode to continue where you left off.

Current board

Insert passage

Long passages can be harder to arrange. You can start with a few verses and expand later.

How to use Notes Mode

  1. Create or select a board in the sidebar.
  2. Click Insert passage and enter a reference (e.g., “Proverbs 1:1–7” or “Romans 8:28”).
  3. Choose whether to split into one block per verse and whether to include verse numbers.
  4. Drag verses and notes around the canvas to build clusters, webs, and outlines.
  5. Export boards when you want a backup or to move them to another device in the future.
