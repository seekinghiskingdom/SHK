Infinite Bible – unified Bible & Boards

Infinite Bible is a single workspace with two main modes: chapter-based Bible boards and free-form boards for topics and notes.

Bible mode

  • One board per chapter, using the shared Bible Tools v1 data.
  • Ideal for repeated work in specific passages (e.g., Genesis 1, Romans 8).
  • Boards only persist if you add your own content (notes, highlights, etc.).

Boards mode

  • Named boards for topics, projects, or sermon prep.
  • Can hold any mix of verse blocks and notes from across Scripture.
  • Also stored locally on the device where you use them.

Notes & limitations

  • No accounts or sync yet; everything is local to one browser.
  • Design and tools are still in early development and may change.
  • You can always fall back to the main Bible Viewer for traditional reading.
