Infinite Bible (Tool)

A visual Bible workspace with two modes: read and explore Scripture chapter by chapter, or build flexible boards of verses and notes.

Open Bible Mode Open Notes Mode

Infinite Bible is in early development. Data is stored locally on your device only (no accounts, no sync).

Two Modes, One Workspace

Infinite Bible (Bible Mode)

Bible Mode treats the Bible as a single, continuous workspace. You load chapters one at a time into a shared “Bible board,” customize each chapter, and move between them as you study.

  • Start with a blank Bible board and add chapters as you need them.
  • Each chapter uses a default template: verses stacked in order, ready for spacing, grouping, and markup.
  • Only the chapter you are working in needs to be loaded at once, so performance stays manageable.
  • Notes and layout changes are stored locally on your device and restored the next time you visit.

Infinite Bible Notes (Notes Mode)

Notes Mode gives you a collection of free-form boards where you can drop any verses you want, arrange them, and surround them with notes, questions, and themes.

  • Create multiple named boards (e.g., “Proverbs Pairs,” “Sermon Prep,” “Theme: Faith and Works”).
  • Add passages or single verses to each board and drag them around the canvas.
  • Attach typed notes nearby to capture insights, questions, and cross-links.
  • Use templates or random verse prompts (planned) to spark new studies.

More About Infinite Bible

How Bible Mode works

Bible Mode is designed for chapter-based study that still feels like one continuous Bible workspace. At first, your Bible board is empty. When you want to study a chapter, you:

  1. Open Bible Mode.
  2. Select a book and chapter (for example, Proverbs 1).
  3. Click to insert the chapter template into the board.
  4. Adjust spacing, group verses, and add notes as needed.

As you move to other chapters (for example, Proverbs 2 the next day), you load those chapters into the same Bible board. The tool can show one chapter at a time (or collapse others) to avoid overloading your device, while still treating the whole thing as a single “Infinite Bible” view.

How Notes Mode works

Notes Mode is closer to a “Bible whiteboard.” You work with multiple boards, each with its own canvas:

  • Create, rename, and delete boards for different topics or projects.
  • Add passages by reference, optionally splitting them into one block per verse.
  • Drag verse blocks and notes into clusters, lines, and webs of connections.
  • Use boards for exegesis, thematic mapping, sermon preparation, or personal reflection.

In v1, everything stays on your device. Future versions may add export/import, templates, stickers, and accounts/sync.

Saving, privacy, and device limits
  • Local only: In early versions, all boards and layouts are saved in your browser (local storage / similar). No data is sent to a server and no account is required.
  • Per-device: Boards live on the device and browser where you use the tool. Using another device or browser will give you a separate, empty set of boards.
  • Limits: For stability, Notes Mode will enforce a soft cap on how many boards you can create on one device.
  • Future features: Export/import and optional cloud sync may be added later so you can back up your boards or move them between devices.
Version status and roadmap

Infinite Bible is being built in stages. Early versions will focus on:

  • Single-board Bible Mode with chapter templates loaded on demand.
  • Notes Mode with basic boards, verse blocks, and typed notes.
  • Local auto-save and restore on the same device.

Later updates may include:

  • Export/import of boards as files.
  • Stickers, markers, and simple visual tags around verses and notes.
  • Bookmarks, table-of-contents views, and chapter templates for quick setup.
  • Account-based sync and sharing (when infrastructure allows).

Tips for Using Infinite Bible

  • Use Bible Mode when you want a single, chapter-organized Bible workspace.
  • Use Notes Mode when you want separate themed boards (e.g., Proverbs pairs, topical studies, sermon notes).
  • Remember that everything is local in early versions—clear browser data with care if you rely on stored boards.
