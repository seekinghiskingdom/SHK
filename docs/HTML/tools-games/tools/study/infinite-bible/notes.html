Infinite Bible Notes – Notes Mode

Build free-form boards of verses and notes, move them around a large canvas, and map out themes, questions, and connections.

Bible Mode Notes Mode

Early version: boards are saved locally in your browser on this device (no accounts or sync).

Your boards

Boards are per-device. For stability, there will be a soft limit on how many boards you can create.

Settings
Quick help
  • Create a new board for each topic or project.
  • Add verses by reference, then drag them into clusters.
  • Add notes around verses to record observations and questions.
  • Boards auto-save locally and reopen where you left off.

Current board

Free-form canvas for verses and notes

Insert passage

Long passages may be slower to insert. You can start with a few verses and expand later.

How to use Notes Mode

  1. Create or select a board from the sidebar.
  2. Click Insert passage to add verses; choose whether to split into one block per verse.
  3. Use Add note to place notes around verses and arrange them on the canvas.
  4. Export a board if you want a backup or to move it to another device in the future.
