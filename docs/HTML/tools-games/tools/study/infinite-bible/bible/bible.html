Infinite Bible – Bible Mode

Work through Scripture one chapter at a time in a single, visual “Infinite Bible” workspace.

Bible Mode Notes Mode

Early version: chapter layouts and notes are saved locally in your browser on this device. No accounts or sync yet.

Chapter controls

Infinite Bible treats all loaded chapters as one Bible board. Only the chapter you focus on has to stay expanded.

Chapters in this Bible board

Click a chapter to jump to it on the canvas. Collapsed chapters stay saved but hidden.

Quick help
  • Use the book and chapter controls above to load chapters into this Bible board.
  • Each chapter can be laid out, annotated, and revisited later.
  • Collapsing a chapter hides it visually but keeps your layout and notes.

Bible board

Visual chapter layouts for the whole Bible, one chapter at a time.

How to use Bible Mode

  1. Choose a Book and Chapter, then click Load chapter.
  2. Decide whether to Make each verse a separate block and whether to Include verse numbers.
  3. On the canvas, pan to move around, and use Select / Move to rearrange verses and notes.
  4. Load new chapters as needed; use the chapter list to jump between them and collapse older chapters.
  5. Your layouts and notes are saved locally and restored the next time you open Bible Mode on this device.
