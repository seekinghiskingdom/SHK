Exporting your study list

Export creates a CSV file with the currently visible entries only.

You can:

  • Open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
  • Sort, tag, or group Strong’s numbers for your own word studies.
  • Save sets of words to re-use later or share with others.
  • In the future, import it into SHK language-learning / study tools.

The CSV includes Strong’s ID, lemma, transliteration, counts, books, top KJV renderings, and an example verse.
