Questions to Consider When Choosing a Church!!!

  • Does the church faithfully preach and teach the Bible?
  • Is the gospel clearly presented and central to the church's teaching and mission?
  • Does the church emphasize discipleship and spiritual growth?
  • Is there a sense of community and fellowship among members?
  • Does the church engage in outreach and service to the local community?
  • Are the church's beliefs and practices aligned with biblical teachings?
  • Do you feel welcomed and comfortable in the church environment?
  • Is there accountability and transparency in church leadership?
  • Does the church hold beliefs and practices that align with ecumenical and historical prescedent?
