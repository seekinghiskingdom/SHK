WHAT IS A CHRISTIAN TESTIMONY? -AI

A TESTIMONY IS A TRUE ACCOUNT OF HOW GOD HAS WORKED IN YOUR LIFE—HOW HE SAVED YOU, ANSWERED PRAYER, PROVIDED IN A NEED, CORRECTED YOU, OR SHOWED HIS POWER AND LOVE IN SPECIFIC WAYS. -AI

SCRIPTURE CALLS BELIEVERS TO BE WITNESSES, SHARING BOTH THE GOSPEL AND THE WAYS GOD IS ACTIVE TODAY SO THAT OTHERS ARE ENCOURAGED, WARNED, AND INVITED TO TRUST JESUS. -AI

THIS HUB ASSUMES THAT EVERY FOLLOWER OF JESUS HAS BOTH A “BIG STORY” (SALVATION TESTIMONY) AND MANY “SMALL STORIES” (DAILY BLESSINGS, PRAYERS, AND MIRACLES) THAT TOGETHER FORM A LIFELONG WITNESS. -AI

HOW TO SHARE YOUR TESTIMONY HERE -AI

TESTIMONIES ON THIS SITE ARE ORGANIZED INTO A FEW MAIN TYPES, ALL UNDER ONE SHARED “TESTIMONY” SYSTEM: PRAYER REQUESTS, ANSWERED PRAYERS, BLESSINGS, MIRACLES, AND GENERAL LIFE TESTIMONIES. -AI

SALVATION TESTIMONY -AI

  • ONE CORE STORY: WHY YOU TRUST JESUS AND HOW HE SAVED YOU. -AI
  • EDITABLE OVER TIME AS YOU GROW, BUT KEPT AS A SINGLE MAIN STORY. -AI

PRAYERS & ANSWERED PRAYERS -AI

  • UNANSWERED PRAYERS CAN BE SAVED (EVEN PRIVATELY) AS YOU WAIT. -AI
  • WHEN GOD ANSWERS, YOU CAN UPDATE THEM INTO PUBLIC TESTIMONIES. -AI

BLESSINGS, MIRACLES & DAILY STORIES -AI

  • RECORD SIMPLE EVERYDAY BLESSINGS AS WELL AS RARE MIRACLES. -AI
  • USE TAGS (E.G., “PROVISION”, “HEALING”, “FAMILY”) TO HELP OTHERS FIND THEM. -AI

IN EARLY VERSIONS, SUBMISSION MAY HAPPEN THROUGH SIMPLE FORMS OR EMAIL-BASED WORKFLOWS, WITH PLANS TO MOVE TO FULLY DYNAMIC ACCOUNT-BASED SUBMISSION AS THE SITE GROWS. -AI

USING THE TESTIMONY WALL (GLOBAL FEED) -AI

THE TESTIMONY WALL IS DESIGNED TO FEEL FAMILIAR LIKE A SIMPLE SOCIAL FEED, BUT WITH A DIFFERENT GOAL: SEEING HOW GOD IS MOVING, NOT COMPARING HUMAN HIGHLIGHTS. -AI

DEFAULT VIEW -AI

  • BY DEFAULT, YOU SEE RECENT PUBLIC TESTIMONIES FROM ROUGHLY THE LAST DAY. -AI
  • IF THERE ARE FEW OR NO ENTRIES, YOU CAN LOAD MORE DAYS OR ENTIRE HISTORY. -AI

FILTERS & TAGS -AI

  • FILTER BY TYPE: PRAYER, BLESSING, MIRACLE, OR GENERAL TESTIMONY. -AI
  • APPLY TAGS (SUCH AS “HEALING”, “FINANCES”, “CAMPUS”, “FAMILY”) TO NARROW WHAT YOU SEE. -AI

FOLLOWED ACCOUNTS (LATER) -AI

  • IN FUTURE VERSIONS, SWITCH TO A VIEW THAT SHOWS ONLY PEOPLE YOU FOLLOW. -AI
  • FOLLOW LISTS WILL BE PRIVATE—NO PUBLIC FOLLOWER COUNTS OR RANKING. -AI

A SIMPLE REPORT / FLAG OPTION WILL BE AVAILABLE ON EACH ENTRY SO MODERATORS CAN REVIEW CONTENT THAT MAY BE HARMFUL, CONFUSING, OR OFF-TOPIC. -AI

MAP & TIMELINE VIEWS (FUTURE FEATURES) -AI

OVER TIME, THE SAME TESTIMONY DATA USED IN THE WALL FEED WILL ALSO POWER VISUAL MODES THAT HELP YOU “SEE” GOD’S WORK IN DIFFERENT WAYS. -AI

BUBBLE / “BLESSINGS JAR” MAP -AI

  • VISUALIZE TESTIMONIES AS BUBBLES GROUPED BY TAGS OR CATEGORIES. -AI
  • LARGER BUBBLES REPRESENT MORE STORIES IN THAT AREA OR THEME. -AI

GEOGRAPHIC MAP MODE -AI

  • OPTIONAL LOCATION FIELDS (COUNTRY / STATE / CITY) CAN FEED FUTURE MAPS. -AI
  • SEE HOW GOD IS MOVING ACROSS REGIONS WITHOUT EXPOSING EXACT ADDRESSES. -AI

TIMELINE VIEW -AI

  • SCROLL A TIMELINE TO EXPLORE TESTIMONIES FROM ANY GIVEN DAY OR RANGE. -AI
  • COMBINE WITH MAP VIEW TO SEE “WHAT GOD WAS DOING” AT A MOMENT IN TIME. -AI

THESE VISUAL MODES MAY APPEAR IN A LATER VERSION, BUT THIS HUB WILL ALWAYS LINK TO THEM AS THEY ARE BUILT OUT. -AI
