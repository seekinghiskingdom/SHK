WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR -AI

THE MISCELLANEOUS LITERATURE PAGE IS INTENTIONALLY LIGHTWEIGHT, FUNCTIONING AS A PLACE TO PUT USEFUL WRITTEN RESOURCES THAT DO NOT YET HAVE A NATURAL CATEGORY OR HUB. -AI

THIS MIGHT INCLUDE ONE-OFF DOCUMENTS, INTERIM GUIDES, EXPERIMENTAL FORMATS, OR REFERENCE TEXTS THAT ARE HELPFUL BUT NOT YET PART OF A MORE POLISHED SERIES. -AI

THE GOAL IS TO KEEP THESE RESOURCES AVAILABLE WITHOUT FORCING THEM INTO CATEGORIES WHERE THEY WOULD CONFUSE READERS OR CLUTTER MORE FOCUSED HUBS. -AI

WHAT KIND OF CONTENT BELONGS HERE? -AI

ITEMS THAT LAND HERE SHOULD BE USEFUL, BUT EITHER TOO NARROW OR TOO MIXED TO FIT CLEANLY INTO EXISTING LITERATURE PAGES. -AI

  • STANDALONE PDF OR TEXT DOCUMENTS THAT DO NOT YET FIT A CLEAR CATEGORY. -AI
  • EARLY OR PROTOTYPE VERSIONS OF GUIDES AND NOTES THAT MAY LATER BECOME FULL ARTICLES OR STUDIES. -AI
  • INTERNAL-STYLE REFERENCE SHEETS THAT ARE STILL HELPFUL FOR PUBLIC READERS (SUCH AS SIMPLE OVERVIEW CHARTS OR CHECKLISTS). -AI
  • ONE-OFF SUMMARIES OR HELPER DOCUMENTS CREATED TO SUPPORT SPECIFIC TOOLS OR PROJECTS. -AI

AS MATERIAL GROWS OR CONNECTS TO OTHER PROJECTS, IT CAN BE MOVED OUT OF THIS SECTION INTO A MORE SPECIFIC HUB. -AI

WHAT SHOULD NOT LIVE HERE LONG-TERM -AI

TO KEEP THIS PAGE FROM BECOMING A PERMANENT DUMPING GROUND, SOME KINDS OF CONTENT SHOULD EITHER SKIP THIS STEP OR PASS THROUGH ONLY BRIEFLY. -AI

  • FULL BIBLE STUDIES OR SERIES THAT CLEARLY BELONG UNDER BIBLE STUDIES OR ARTICLES. -AI
  • CHILDREN-FOCUSED MATERIALS THAT BELONG UNDER CHILDREN’S LITERATURE OR CHILDREN’S RESOURCES. -AI
  • HISTORICAL TEXTS THAT ARE BETTER CLASSIFIED UNDER HISTORICAL WRITINGS, CREEDS, COUNCILS, OR FATHERS. -AI
  • LONG-FORM BOOK PROJECTS THAT SHOULD BE TRACKED IN THE BOOKS & READING LISTS SECTION. -AI

WHEN IN DOUBT, IT IS USUALLY BETTER TO CREATE A SMALL, CLEAR HUB OR SUBPAGE THAN TO LEAVE A MATURE RESOURCE PERMANENTLY IN THE MISCELLANEOUS CATEGORY. -AI

TEMPORARY PARKING AREA FOR NEW RESOURCES -AI

PRACTICALLY, THIS PAGE CAN BE USED AS A SHORT-TERM HOLDING AREA WHILE NEW WRITTEN MATERIAL IS BEING TESTED, ORGANIZED, OR WAITING FOR A FINAL CATEGORY DECISION. -AI

SAMPLE USES OF THIS SECTION MIGHT INCLUDE: -AI

  • UPLOADING A NEW GUIDE OR NOTESET AND LINKING IT HERE BEFORE CREATING A FORMAL HUB. -AI
  • STORING INTERNAL-STYLE REFERENCE TEXTS THAT YOU DECIDE ARE HELPFUL TO MAKE PUBLIC. -AI
  • COLLECTING ONE-OFF EXPLANATIONS OR SUMMARY DOCUMENTS THAT SUPPORT A TOOL OR MINISTRY AREA. -AI

INTERNALLY, YOU CAN TREAT THIS SECTION AS A “TO SORT LATER” LIST FOR LITERATURE, WHILE STILL GIVING USERS ACCESS TO THE CONTENT. -AI

FUTURE RECLASSIFICATION & CLEANUP -AI

AS THE SITE GROWS, IT WILL BE HEALTHY TO REVIEW THIS PAGE FROM TIME TO TIME AND MOVE MATERIALS INTO MORE SPECIFIC HUBS WHEN THEIR PURPOSE BECOMES CLEAR. -AI

  • PERIODICALLY REVIEW ITEMS HERE AND DECIDE WHETHER THEY SHOULD BE MOVED OR EXPANDED. -AI
  • CREATE NEW HUBS OR SUBPAGES WHEN MULTIPLE “MISC” ITEMS START TO SHARE A CLEAR THEME. -AI
  • RENAME OR ARCHIVE OUTDATED RESOURCES SO USERS ARE NOT CONFUSED BY OLD DRAFTS. -AI

EDITOR NOTE: WHEN YOU MOVE A RESOURCE OUT OF THIS SECTION INTO A MORE SPECIFIC PAGE, UPDATE ANY LINKS AND REMOVE THE -AI SUFFIX FROM TEXT THAT HAS BEEN REVIEWED. -AI
