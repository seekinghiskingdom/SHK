TYPES OF CHILDREN’S LITERATURE & MATERIALS -AI

DIFFERENT TYPES OF WRITTEN RESOURCES CAN SERVE CHILDREN IN DIFFERENT WAYS, FROM SIMPLE STORYTELLING TO MORE STRUCTURED DEVOTIONAL READING. -AI

BIBLE STORYBOOKS -AI

  • RETELLINGS OF KEY BIBLICAL STORIES IN CHILD-FRIENDLY LANGUAGE, OFTEN WITH ILLUSTRATIONS. -AI
  • HELP CHILDREN BECOME FAMILIAR WITH MAJOR EVENTS AND CHARACTERS IN SCRIPTURE. -AI

DEVOTIONALS FOR CHILDREN -AI

  • SHORT DAILY OR WEEKLY READINGS THAT COMBINE SCRIPTURE, EXPLANATION, AND SIMPLE APPLICATION. -AI
  • CAN BE READ BY OLDER CHILDREN OR SHARED ALOUD BY ADULTS WITH YOUNGER ONES. -AI

ACTIVITY & WORKBOOK MATERIALS -AI

  • PRINTABLE PAGES OR BOOKLETS WITH COLORING, PUZZLES, OR SIMPLE QUESTIONS BASED ON BIBLE PASSAGES. -AI
  • USEFUL FOR REINFORCING STORIES THROUGH HANDS-ON ACTIVITY. -AI

FAMILY READ-ALOUD BOOKS -AI

  • LONGER STORIES OR SERIES THAT FAMILIES CAN READ TOGETHER OVER MANY DAYS. -AI
  • OFTEN MIX NARRATIVE WITH THEMES SUCH AS FAITH, OBEDIENCE, AND GOD’S CHARACTER. -AI

TEACHER & LEADER GUIDES -AI

  • MATERIALS THAT HELP PARENTS, TEACHERS, OR MINISTRY LEADERS PREPARE BIBLE LESSONS FOR CHILDREN. -AI
  • CAN INCLUDE SAMPLE LESSON PLANS, QUESTIONS, AND SIMPLE OBJECT LESSON IDEAS. -AI

EARLY READER SCRIPTURE PORTIONS -AI

  • SIMPLE ADAPTATIONS OR CAREFULLY CHOSEN PASSAGES TO HELP EARLY READERS PRACTICE READING WITH BIBLE-RELATED TEXT. -AI
  • CAN CONNECT DIRECTLY BACK TO FULL BIBLE PASSAGES IN A REGULAR TRANSLATION. -AI

AGE RANGES & LEVELS -AI

WHEN RECOMMENDING OR ORGANIZING CHILDREN’S LITERATURE, IT HELPS TO ROUGHLY MARK WHICH AGE GROUPS EACH RESOURCE IS BEST SUITED FOR. -AI

YOUNG CHILDREN (APPROX. 3–6) -AI

  • PICTURE-HEAVY STORYBOOKS, SIMPLE RETELLINGS, SHORT DEVOTIONAL THOUGHTS, AND COLORING PAGES. -AI
  • FOCUS ON BASIC TRUTHS ABOUT GOD’S LOVE, GOD’S CREATION, AND SIMPLE BIBLE STORIES. -AI

EARLY READERS (APPROX. 6–9) -AI

  • BEGINNER READER BOOKS, SHORT CHAPTER STORIES, AND SIMPLE QUESTION-AND-ANSWER DEVOTIONALS. -AI
  • INTRODUCE CLEAR CONNECTIONS BETWEEN STORIES AND BIBLE PASSAGES. -AI

OLDER CHILDREN (APPROX. 9–12) -AI

  • LONGER STORIES, MORE DETAILED DEVOTIONALS, AND BASIC THEME-FOCUSED BOOKS. -AI
  • CAN BEGIN TO EXPLORE HOW BIBLE THEMES RELATE TO DAILY LIFE, QUESTIONS, AND STRUGGLES. -AI

FLEXIBLE & FAMILY-SHARED -AI

  • RESOURCES THAT CAN BE ADAPTED UP OR DOWN BY HOW THEY ARE READ OR DISCUSSED. -AI
  • SUITABLE FOR MIXED-AGE SETTINGS WHEN PARENTS OR LEADERS GUIDE THE CONVERSATION. -AI

FAMILY READING & DISCUSSION IDEAS -AI

CHILDREN’S LITERATURE CAN BE MOST FRUITFUL WHEN IT IS WRAPPED IN REGULAR FAMILY RHYTHMS OF READING, DISCUSSION, AND PRAYER. -AI

SHORT DAILY RHYTHMS -AI

  • READ ONE SHORT STORY OR DEVOTIONAL EACH DAY, THEN ASK ONE OR TWO SIMPLE QUESTIONS. -AI
  • CLOSE WITH A SHORT PRAYER CONNECTED TO THE STORY OR PASSAGE. -AI

WEEKLY FOCUS THEMES -AI

  • CHOOSE A SIMPLE THEME (SUCH AS THANKFULNESS OR TRUST) AND READ SEVERAL STORIES THAT RELATE TO IT OVER A WEEK. -AI
  • HELP CHILDREN NOTICE HOW DIFFERENT STORIES POINT TO THE SAME CHARACTER OF GOD. -AI

CONNECTING TO SCRIPTURE -AI

  • WHENEVER POSSIBLE, SHOW CHILDREN WHERE A STORY OR DEVOTIONAL CONNECTS TO A SPECIFIC BIBLE PASSAGE. -AI
  • AS THEY GROW, INVITE THEM TO READ OR LISTEN TO THE BIBLICAL TEXT ITSELF. -AI

CREATIVE FOLLOW-UP -AI

  • LET CHILDREN DRAW, ACT OUT, OR SUMMARIZE STORIES IN THEIR OWN WORDS AFTER READING. -AI
  • USE SIMPLE ACTIVITIES TO HELP TRUTH SINK IN THROUGH MORE THAN WORDS ALONE. -AI
