ABOUT STRONG’S CONCORDANCE -AI

STRONG’S CONCORDANCE WAS DESIGNED TO HELP READERS MOVE FROM TRANSLATED WORDS BACK TO THEIR UNDERLYING HEBREW OR GREEK LEMMAS, WITHOUT REQUIRING FULL TRAINING IN BIBLICAL LANGUAGES. -AI

ON THIS SITE, STRONG’S NUMBERS ACT AS SIMPLE HANDLES: FROM A VERSE VIEW OR TOOL, YOU CAN FOLLOW AN H- OR G-NUMBER TO ITS ENTRY PAGE, SEE THE ORIGINAL-WORD FORM, AND BEGIN BASIC WORD STUDY. -AI

EACH ENTRY CAN EVENTUALLY INCLUDE A LEMMA, A SHORT GLOSS, BASIC PRONUNCIATION, AND LINKS TO PASSAGES OR TOOLS THAT USE THAT WORD, LEAVING ROOM FOR FUTURE GROWTH AND CROSS-CONNECTIONS. -AI

STRONG’S LEXICONS -AI

STRONG’S NUMBERS ARE GROUPED INTO TWO MAIN SETS: HEBREW (H-NUMBERS) FOR THE OLD TESTAMENT AND GREEK (G-NUMBERS) FOR THE NEW TESTAMENT. -AI

HEBREW LEXICON (H-NUMBERS) -AI

THE HEBREW STRONG’S LEXICON COVERS WORDS PRIMARILY FROM THE OLD TESTAMENT, WRITTEN IN BIBLICAL HEBREW (WITH SOME ARAMAIC), A CONSONANT-RICH SEMITIC LANGUAGE USED BY ANCIENT ISRAEL. -AI

  • EACH ENTRY IS TAGGED WITH AN H-NUMBER (E.G., H0001). -AI
  • ENTRIES CONNECT ENGLISH RENDERINGS BACK TO A SPECIFIC HEBREW LEMMA. -AI
  • USEFUL AS A STARTING POINT FOR WORD STUDIES AND CROSS-REFERENCES. -AI

GREEK LEXICON (G-NUMBERS) -AI

THE GREEK STRONG’S LEXICON COVERS WORDS FROM THE NEW TESTAMENT, WRITTEN IN KOINE GREEK, THE COMMON DIALECT OF THE EASTERN MEDITERRANEAN IN THE FIRST CENTURY. -AI

  • EACH ENTRY IS TAGGED WITH A G-NUMBER (E.G., G0001). -AI
  • ENTRIES CONNECT TRANSLATED WORDS TO THEIR UNDERLYING GREEK LEMMAS. -AI
  • HELPS YOU TRACE HOW A TERM IS USED ACROSS MULTIPLE PASSAGES. -AI

USING STRONG’S NUMBERS ON THIS SITE -AI

STRONG’S NUMBERS ARE BUILT INTO VARIOUS TOOLS SO THAT YOU CAN MOVE BACK AND FORTH BETWEEN VERSES, WORD ENTRIES, AND OTHER RESOURCES WITHOUT LOSING YOUR PLACE. -AI

  • WHEN YOU SEE A STRONG’S NUMBER NEXT TO A WORD OR PHRASE, YOU CAN OPEN THAT ENTRY TO VIEW THE LEMMA, BASIC GLOSS, AND RELATED INFO. -AI
  • USE THE HEBREW AND GREEK INDEX PAGES TO BROWSE OR SEARCH FOR SPECIFIC H- OR G-NUMBERS WHEN DOING MORE FOCUSED WORD STUDIES. -AI
  • TREAT STRONG’S AS A STARTING POINT: IT POINTS YOU TOWARD THE ORIGINAL WORD, BUT THE FULL MEANING STILL DEPENDS ON CONTEXT, GRAMMAR, AND THE FLOW OF THE PASSAGE. -AI

STRONG’S ENTRIES ARE DESIGNED TO CONNECT WITH OTHER BIBLE TOOLS, SO THAT LANGUAGE INFORMATION CAN SUPPORT REGULAR STUDY RATHER THAN SITTING OFF TO THE SIDE. -AI

CONCORDANCE & SEARCH TOOLS -AI

WHERE TO START WITH STRONG’S -AI

WHETHER YOU ARE BRAND NEW TO STRONG’S OR HAVE USED IT BEFORE, THESE SIMPLE PATHS CAN HELP YOU INTEGRATE IT INTO YOUR REGULAR BIBLE STUDY. -AI

I HAVE NEVER USED STRONG’S BEFORE -AI

  • START BY NOTICING WHEN H- OR G-NUMBERS APPEAR NEXT TO WORDS IN VERSE-BASED TOOLS. -AI
  • OPEN A FEW ENTRIES JUST TO SEE HOW THEY LOOK AND WHAT INFORMATION THEY PROVIDE. -AI

FOCUS ON A FEW KEY WORDS IN A FAVORITE PASSAGE RATHER THAN TRYING TO LOOK UP EVERYTHING AT ONCE. -AI

I WANT TO DO SIMPLE WORD STUDIES -AI

  • CHOOSE ONE H- OR G-NUMBER FROM A PASSAGE AND FOLLOW IT THROUGH OTHER VERSES THAT USE THE SAME ENTRY. -AI
  • COMPARE HOW TRANSLATIONS RENDER THAT WORD IN DIFFERENT CONTEXTS. -AI

LET THESE COMPARISONS SEND YOU BACK TO THE PASSAGES THEMSELVES, WATCHING HOW CONTEXT SHAPES MEANING. -AI

I USE LANGUAGE TOOLS REGULARLY -AI

  • INTEGRATE STRONG’S ENTRIES WITH OTHER RESOURCES, SUCH AS LEXICONS OR GRAMMAR NOTES, WHERE AVAILABLE. -AI
  • USE STRONG’S AS AN INDEX, NOT AS YOUR ONLY SOURCE FOR MEANING OR THEOLOGICAL CONCLUSIONS. -AI

KEEP SCRIPTURE CENTRAL, AND LET LANGUAGE TOOLS SERVE CLARITY, HUMILITY, AND OBEDIENCE. -AI
