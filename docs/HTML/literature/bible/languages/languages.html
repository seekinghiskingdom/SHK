WHY BIBLICAL LANGUAGES MATTER -AI

THE OLD AND NEW TESTAMENTS WERE WRITTEN IN ANCIENT LANGUAGES WHOSE WORDS, GRAMMAR, AND IDIOMS SOMETIMES CARRY NUANCE THAT CAN BE FLATTENED OR OBSCURED IN TRANSLATION. -AI

STUDYING BIBLICAL LANGUAGES—EVEN AT A VERY BASIC LEVEL—CAN HELP CLARIFY MEANINGS, SEE PATTERNS, AND NOTICE CONNECTIONS ACROSS SCRIPTURE THAT MAY NOT BE AS OBVIOUS IN A SINGLE TRANSLATION. -AI
