BOOK CATEGORIES -AI

TO KEEP THINGS CLEAR AND APPROACHABLE, BOOKS CAN BE GROUPED INTO A FEW SIMPLE CATEGORIES BASED ON FOCUS AND APPROXIMATE DEPTH. -AI

INTRODUCTORY BOOKS -AI

  • ACCESSIBLE WORKS SUITABLE FOR NEW BELIEVERS OR THOSE NEW TO A TOPIC. -AI
  • FOCUS ON CLEAR LANGUAGE, BASIC FRAMEWORKS, AND STRAIGHTFORWARD APPLICATION. -AI

INTERMEDIATE BOOKS -AI

  • WORKS THAT ASSUME SOME FAMILIARITY WITH SCRIPTURE OR BASIC DOCTRINE. -AI
  • OFTEN GO DEEPER INTO ARGUMENTS, HISTORY, OR PRACTICE WHILE REMAINING READABLE. -AI

ADVANCED & SPECIALIZED BOOKS -AI

  • MORE DETAILED OR TECHNICAL STUDIES, INCLUDING SCHOLARLY OR SPECIALIZED TOPICS. -AI
  • BEST USED AFTER BUILDING A STRONG FOUNDATION THROUGH SCRIPTURE AND SIMPLER WORKS. -AI

TOPIC-FOCUSED BOOKS -AI

  • BOOKS ORGANIZED AROUND THEMES SUCH AS PRAYER, SUFFERING, MISSION, OR DISCIPLESHIP. -AI
  • OFTEN USE MANY PASSAGES FROM ACROSS THE BIBLE TO ADDRESS ONE AREA OF LIFE. -AI

HISTORICAL & BIOGRAPHICAL BOOKS -AI

  • WRITINGS THAT TELL THE STORIES OF BELIEVERS, MOVEMENTS, OR EVENTS IN CHURCH HISTORY. -AI
  • CAN OFTEN BE LINKED TO PRIMARY HISTORICAL TEXTS IN THE HISTORICAL WRITINGS SECTION. -AI

CHILDREN & FAMILY-ORIENTED BOOKS -AI

  • BOOKS WRITTEN FOR CHILDREN OR FOR FAMILIES TO READ TOGETHER. -AI

HOW TO USE THESE BOOK RESOURCES -AI

BOOKS CAN SUPPORT YOUR GROWTH, BUT THEY ARE MOST HELPFUL WHEN THEY STRENGTHEN YOUR DIRECT ENGAGEMENT WITH SCRIPTURE AND YOUR INVOLVEMENT IN A LOCAL CHURCH COMMUNITY. -AI

KEEP SCRIPTURE PRIMARY -AI

  • LET BOOKS DRIVE YOU BACK TO THE BIBLE, NOT REPLACE YOUR OWN READING OF THE TEXT. -AI
  • LOOK UP PASSAGES AND CONTEXTS CITED IN THE BOOKS YOU ARE READING. -AI

PACE YOURSELF WISELY -AI

  • IT IS BETTER TO READ SLOWLY WITH UNDERSTANDING THAN QUICKLY WITHOUT REFLECTION. -AI
  • CHOOSE ONE OR TWO BOOKS AT A TIME RATHER THAN STACKING TOO MANY AT ONCE. -AI

DISCUSS WITH OTHERS -AI

  • WHEN POSSIBLE, READ WITH FRIENDS, A SMALL GROUP, OR MENTORS WHO CAN HELP YOU PROCESS WHAT YOU ARE LEARNING. -AI
  • USE BOOKS AS STARTING POINTS FOR CONVERSATION, NOT FINAL AUTHORITIES. -AI

CHECK SOURCES & BALANCE -AI

  • OVER TIME, READ AUTHORS FROM DIFFERENT PERIODS AND CONTEXTS TO SEE A BROADER RANGE OF INSIGHT. -AI
  • ALWAYS WEIGH WHAT YOU READ AGAINST SCRIPTURE, TRUSTING GOD’S WORD ABOVE ANY HUMAN AUTHOR. -AI
