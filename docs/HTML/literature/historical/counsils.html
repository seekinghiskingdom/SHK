HOW TO USE COUNCIL TEXTS WISELY -AI

READING COUNCIL DOCUMENTS CAN HELP US SEE HOW EARLIER CHRISTIANS STRUGGLED WITH IMPORTANT QUESTIONS, BUT THEY ARE BEST APPROACHED WITH CARE, CONTEXT, AND SCRIPTURE IN VIEW. -AI

KEEP SCRIPTURE PRIMARY -AI

  • LET THE BIBLE REMAIN YOUR PRIMARY AUTHORITY, AND READ COUNCIL TEXTS AS HISTORICAL AND THEOLOGICAL WITNESS RATHER THAN REPLACEMENTS FOR SCRIPTURE. -AI
  • NOTICE HOW THESE GATHERINGS APPEAL TO SCRIPTURE AND WHAT PASSAGES THEY EMPHASIZE. -AI

PAY ATTENTION TO CONTEXT -AI

  • COUNCILS RESPOND TO SPECIFIC DISPUTES, CULTURES, AND MOMENTS IN HISTORY, WHICH SHAPE THEIR LANGUAGE AND PRIORITIES. -AI
  • BRIEF HISTORICAL INTRODUCTIONS CAN HELP MAKE THEIR DECISIONS EASIER TO UNDERSTAND. -AI

RECOGNIZE DIVERSE RECEPTIONS -AI

  • DIFFERENT CHRISTIAN TRADITIONS RECEIVE SOME COUNCILS AND THEIR DECISIONS IN DIFFERENT WAYS. -AI
  • IT CAN BE HELPFUL TO LEARN HOW YOUR OWN CHURCH OR TRADITION RELATES TO PARTICULAR COUNCILS. -AI
