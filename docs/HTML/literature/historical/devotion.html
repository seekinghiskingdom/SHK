HOW TO READ DEVOTIONAL WRITINGS WISELY -AI

DEVOTIONAL LITERATURE CAN BE A GREAT GIFT WHEN IT LEADS US TOWARD SCRIPTURE, PRAYER, AND OBEDIENCE, BUT IT IS BEST APPROACHED WITH DISCERNMENT AND CLEAR PRIORITIES. -AI

KEEP THE BIBLE CENTRAL -AI

  • LET DEVOTIONAL WRITINGS POINT YOU BACK TO SCRIPTURE RATHER THAN REPLACE IT. -AI
  • WHENEVER POSSIBLE, READ DEVOTIONALS ALONGSIDE THE PASSAGES THEY MENTION OR REFLECT ON. -AI

NOTICE EMPHASES & LIMITS -AI

  • EVERY WRITER HAS PARTICULAR EMPHASES AND BLIND SPOTS; NO SINGLE BOOK CAPTURES THE FULL RICHNESS OF THE BIBLE’S TEACHING. -AI
  • USE MULTIPLE RESOURCES OVER TIME RATHER THAN RESTING EVERYTHING ON ONE FAVORITE AUTHOR. -AI

READ WITH PRAYER & COMMUNITY -AI

  • ASK GOD TO USE WHAT YOU READ TO DRAW YOU CLOSER TO HIM AND TO SHAPE YOUR HEART AND HABITS. -AI
  • CONSIDER DISCUSSING IMPACTFUL PASSAGES WITH FRIENDS, MENTORS, OR YOUR CHURCH COMMUNITY. -AI
