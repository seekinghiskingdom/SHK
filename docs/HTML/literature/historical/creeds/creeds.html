WHAT ARE CREEDS & CONFESSIONS? -AI

CREEDS AND CONFESSIONS ARE SHORT, CAREFULLY WORDED STATEMENTS THAT SUMMARIZE CENTRAL BIBLICAL TEACHINGS, OFTEN SHAPED IN TIMES WHEN THE CHURCH NEEDED TO CLARIFY OR DEFEND THE FAITH. -AI

THEY ARE NOT MEANT TO REPLACE SCRIPTURE, BUT TO POINT BACK TO IT, GATHERING KEY TRUTHS INTO MEMORABLE, SHARED LANGUAGE THAT CAN BE USED IN TEACHING, WORSHIP, AND DISCIPLESHIP. -AI

THIS PAGE PROVIDES A SIMPLE ORIENTATION TO SEVERAL HISTORIC CREEDS AND MAKES ROOM TO ADD FURTHER CONFESSIONS AND RELATED DOCUMENTS OVER TIME. -AI

NICENE CREED -AI

A CENTRAL CREED THAT EMPHASIZES THE FULL DIVINITY OF JESUS CHRIST AND THE TRINITARIAN NATURE OF GOD, FORMULATED IN THE CONTEXT OF EARLY COUNCILS. -AI

ATHANASIAN CREED -AI

A DETAILED STATEMENT THAT EXPLAINS THE TRINITY AND THE PERSON OF CHRIST WITH UNUSUAL THOROUGHNESS, STRESSING BOTH UNITY AND DISTINCTIONS WITHIN THE GODHEAD. -AI

  • OFFERS CAREFUL LANGUAGE ABOUT FATHER, SON, AND HOLY SPIRIT. -AI
  • CLARIFIES HOW JESUS IS BOTH FULLY GOD AND FULLY HUMAN. -AI

CHALCEDONIAN DEFINITION -AI

A CLASSIC FORMULATION ON THE PERSON OF CHRIST, AFFIRMING THAT HE IS ONE PERSON IN TWO NATURES, FULLY DIVINE AND FULLY HUMAN, WITHOUT CONFUSION OR DIVISION. -AI

HOW CREEDS ARE USED -AI

CREEDS HAVE BEEN USED IN MANY WAYS THROUGHOUT CHRISTIAN HISTORY, OFTEN SERVING BOTH AS TEACHING TOOLS AND AS EXPRESSIONS OF SHARED FAITH IN WORSHIP. -AI

TEACHING & CATECHESIS -AI

  • CREEDS PROVIDE A SIMPLE STRUCTURE FOR TEACHING BASIC BIBLICAL TRUTHS. -AI
  • THEY CAN BE MEMORIZED AND USED AS FRAMEWORKS FOR EXPLORING SCRIPTURE. -AI

WORSHIP & PUBLIC CONFESSION -AI

  • MANY CHURCHES RECITE CREEDS TOGETHER AS A SHARED CONFESSION OF FAITH. -AI
  • THIS PRACTICE CONNECTS PRESENT BELIEVERS WITH FAITHFUL WITNESSES FROM EARLIER CENTURIES. -AI

GUARDING SOUND TEACHING -AI

  • CREEDS HELP MARK OUT CENTRAL BIBLICAL TEACHINGS IN THE MIDST OF CONFUSION OR ERROR. -AI
  • THEY CAN BE USED AS REFERENCE POINTS WHEN QUESTIONS ABOUT CORE DOCTRINES ARISE. -AI
