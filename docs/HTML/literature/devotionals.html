WHAT ARE DEVOTIONALS & PRACTICE RESOURCES? -AI

DEVOTIONALS AND PRACTICE RESOURCES ARE WRITINGS, GUIDES, OR SERIES DESIGNED TO HELP YOU MEET WITH GOD REGULARLY, REFLECT ON SCRIPTURE, AND GROW IN DAILY OBEDIENCE. -AI

THEY CAN TAKE MANY FORMS, SUCH AS SHORT DAILY READINGS, THEMATIC SERIES, TOPICAL GUIDES, OR SEASONAL JOURNEYS THAT CONNECT BIBLICAL TRUTH WITH ORDINARY LIFE. -AI

THIS PAGE IS A FRAMEWORK WHERE SPECIFIC DEVOTIONAL SERIES, READING PLANS, AND PRACTICE-FOCUSED GUIDES CAN BE GATHERED OVER TIME, ALONG WITH LINKS TO TRUSTED EXTERNAL RESOURCES. -AI

TYPES OF DEVOTIONAL RESOURCES -AI

MODERN DEVOTIONAL MATERIALS CAN BE ORGANIZED INTO SEVERAL BROAD CATEGORIES, EACH SERVING DIFFERENT RHYTHMS AND NEEDS. -AI

DAILY OR WEEKLY DEVOTIONAL SERIES -AI

  • SHORT READINGS BUILT AROUND SCRIPTURE, USUALLY WITH A REFLECTION AND A PRAYER OR RESPONSE. -AI
  • OFTEN ARRANGED BY DATE, MAKING IT EASY TO FOLLOW A SIMPLE RHYTHM OVER TIME. -AI

TOPICAL & THEMATIC DEVOTIONALS -AI

  • FOCUS ON SPECIFIC THEMES (SUCH AS HOPE, SUFFERING, PRAYER, OR MISSION) OVER A SET NUMBER OF READINGS. -AI
  • USEFUL FOR SHORT STUDIES, SMALL GROUPS, OR SEASONS OF LIFE WHERE CERTAIN TOPICS ARE PROMINENT. -AI

SEASONAL & JOURNEY DEVOTIONALS -AI

  • DESIGNED FOR PARTICULAR SEASONS OR JOURNEYS (SUCH AS PREPARING FOR BAPTISM, WALKING THROUGH GRIEF, OR SPECIFIC CHURCH SEASONS WHERE APPLICABLE). -AI
  • CAN HELP FRAME A PARTICULAR PERIOD OF LIFE AROUND SCRIPTURE, PRAYER, AND INTENTIONAL REFLECTION. -AI

HOW TO USE DEVOTIONALS WISELY -AI

DEVOTIONALS ARE MEANT TO SUPPORT YOUR DIRECT ENGAGEMENT WITH GOD AND HIS WORD, NOT TO REPLACE SCRIPTURE ITSELF. -AI

KEEP SCRIPTURE CENTRAL -AI

  • READ THE PASSAGE ITSELF, NOT ONLY THE REFLECTION OR COMMENTARY. -AI
  • ASK WHAT THE TEXT SAYS, WHAT IT MEANS, AND HOW IT INVITES YOU TO RESPOND. -AI

USE DEVOTIONALS AS HELPS, NOT REPLACEMENTS -AI

  • LET DEVOTIONAL MATERIALS POINT YOU BACK TO THE BIBLE AND TO PRAYER, RATHER THAN BECOMING YOUR ONLY SOURCE. -AI
  • IF A DEVOTIONAL READING IS SHORT, CONSIDER READING MORE OF THE PASSAGE AROUND IT. -AI

ADJUST TO YOUR SEASON -AI

  • IN BUSY OR HEAVY SEASONS, SHORT READINGS AND SIMPLE RHYTHMS MAY BE MORE REALISTIC. -AI
  • IN SLOWER SEASONS, YOU MAY CHOOSE LONGER READINGS OR MULTIPLE RESOURCES IN PARALLEL. -AI

PRACTICES & HABITS FOR SEEKING GOD -AI

MANY DEVOTIONAL RESOURCES ARE CONNECTED TO SIMPLE PRACTICES THAT CAN BE WOVEN INTO DAILY LIFE. -AI

DAILY SCRIPTURE & PRAYER RHYTHMS -AI

  • SET ASIDE REGULAR, REALISTIC TIMES—EVEN SHORT ONES—to READ SCRIPTURE AND PRAY. -AI
  • PAIR READINGS WITH SIMPLE PRAYER PROMPTS OR JOURNALING, WHERE HELPFUL. -AI

SILENCE, REFLECTION & THANKFULNESS -AI

  • LEAVE SPACE FOR QUIET REFLECTION RATHER THAN MOVING IMMEDIATELY FROM READING TO TASKS. -AI
  • PRACTICE NOTICING AND THANKING GOD FOR SPECIFIC GIFTS, ANSWERS, OR ENCOURAGEMENTS. -AI

SHARED DEVOTIONAL PRACTICES -AI

  • CONSIDER WALKING THROUGH A DEVOTIONAL SERIES WITH A FRIEND, FAMILY MEMBER, OR SMALL GROUP. -AI
  • USE QUESTIONS OR PROMPTS FROM DEVOTIONALS TO START CONVERSATIONS ABOUT FAITH. -AI

FUTURE CONTENT: PRINTABLE PRACTICE SHEETS, SIMPLE TRACKERS, AND SAMPLE RHYTHMS FOR DIFFERENT SEASONS OF LIFE. -AI
