WHAT ARE COMMENTARIES & STUDY HELPS? -AI

BIBLE COMMENTARIES, STUDY NOTES, AND OTHER HELPS ARE WRITINGS WHERE TEACHERS AND SCHOLARS TRY TO EXPLAIN PASSAGES, CLARIFY BACKGROUND, AND CONNECT VERSES TO BROADER BIBLICAL THEMES. -AI

THEY CAN BE VERY USEFUL FOR UNDERSTANDING HISTORICAL CONTEXT, DIFFICULT PHRASES, AND DIFFERENT INTERPRETIVE OPTIONS, BUT THEY ARE STILL HUMAN REFLECTIONS AND MUST BE WEIGHED AGAINST SCRIPTURE. -AI

THIS PAGE IS MEANT TO HELP YOU KNOW WHAT THESE RESOURCES ARE, HOW TO APPROACH THEM, AND WHERE TO FIND THEM, RATHER THAN TO PUSH ANY PARTICULAR COMMENTARY SET OR TRADITION. -AI

TYPES OF COMMENTARIES & STUDY RESOURCES -AI

NOT ALL COMMENTARIES OR STUDY HELPS ARE THE SAME; DIFFERENT KINDS SERVE DIFFERENT PURPOSES AND ARE WRITTEN AT DIFFERENT LEVELS OF DEPTH. -AI

SINGLE-VOLUME BIBLE COMMENTARIES -AI

  • OVERVIEW WORKS THAT COVER THE ENTIRE BIBLE IN ONE VOLUME WITH BRIEF NOTES ON EACH BOOK OR PASSAGE. -AI
  • USEFUL FOR QUICK ORIENTATION AND BASIC QUESTIONS WHEN YOU DO NOT NEED EXTENSIVE DETAIL. -AI

MULTI-VOLUME OR SERIES COMMENTARIES -AI

  • SETS THAT DEVOTE A FULL VOLUME OR MORE TO A SINGLE BOOK OR SMALL GROUP OF BIBLICAL BOOKS. -AI
  • OFTEN PROVIDE DEEPER DISCUSSION OF LANGUAGE, CONTEXT, AND THEOLOGICAL THEMES. -AI

STUDY BIBLES & MARGIN NOTES -AI

  • BIBLES THAT INCLUDE NOTES, CHARTS, MAPS, AND INTRODUCTIONS ALONGSIDE THE TEXT. -AI
  • CONVENIENT FOR CARRYING BASIC HELPS WITH YOU, WHILE REMEMBERING THAT NOTES ARE NOT THE BIBLICAL TEXT ITSELF. -AI

BIBLE HANDBOOKS & SURVEYS -AI

  • OVERVIEW RESOURCES THAT INTRODUCE EACH BIBLE BOOK, OUTLINE MAIN THEMES, AND HIGHLIGHT KEY PASSAGES. -AI
  • HELPFUL FOR SEEING THE BIG PICTURE BEFORE OR AFTER READING A BOOK OF THE BIBLE. -AI

BACKGROUND & WORD-STUDY RESOURCES -AI

  • TOOLS THAT FOCUS ON HISTORICAL, CULTURAL, OR LINGUISTIC DETAILS, SUCH AS BIBLE DICTIONARIES OR WORD-STUDY GUIDES. -AI
  • ESPECIALLY HELPFUL WHEN PAIRED WITH BIBLICAL LANGUAGE TOOLS AND STRONG’S NUMBERS. -AI

DEVOTIONAL COMMENTARIES -AI

  • RESOURCES THAT EMPHASIZE PERSONAL APPLICATION AND ENCOURAGEMENT WHILE STILL WALKING THROUGH THE TEXT. -AI
  • OFTEN USE SHORT READINGS THAT COMBINE EXPOSITION WITH PRAYERFUL REFLECTION. -AI

HOW TO USE COMMENTARIES & STUDY HELPS WELL -AI

COMMENTARIES ARE MOST HELPFUL WHEN THEY SUPPORT CAREFUL, PRAYERFUL READING OF SCRIPTURE RATHER THAN REPLACING IT OR SHORT-CIRCUITING YOUR OWN STUDY. -AI

READ SCRIPTURE FIRST -AI

  • START BY READING THE PASSAGE YOURSELF, MAYBE MORE THAN ONCE, BEFORE OPENING NOTES OR COMMENTARIES. -AI
  • NOTICE QUESTIONS, PATTERNS, AND CONNECTIONS AS YOU READ; THEN SEE HOW COMMENTARIES ADDRESS THEM. -AI

COMPARE, DO NOT COPY -AI

  • USE COMMENTARIES TO COMPARE INTERPRETATIONS AND REASONS GIVEN, NOT JUST TO BORROW CONCLUSIONS. -AI
  • WHEN POSSIBLE, CHECK THE BIBLICAL TEXT AND CROSS-REFERENCES FOR YOURSELF. -AI

BALANCE DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES -AI

  • OVER TIME, CONSULT MORE THAN ONE RESOURCE SO YOU SEE HOW DIFFERENT WRITERS HANDLE THE SAME PASSAGE. -AI
  • LOOK FOR POINTS OF AGREEMENT ON CLEAR MATTERS AND HUMILITY WHERE SCRIPTURE LEAVES ROOM FOR TENSION. -AI

KEEP THE GOAL IN VIEW -AI

  • LET COMMENTARIES SERVE LOVE FOR GOD AND NEIGHBOR, NOT JUST INFORMATION OR ARGUMENTS. -AI
  • ASK HOW INSIGHTS FROM THESE RESOURCES CAN LEAD TO WORSHIP, REPENTANCE, AND OBEDIENCE. -AI
